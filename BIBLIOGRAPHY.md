# Bibliography

*Every external work this project cites, reproduces, depends on, or corrects has an entry here, added the day it
is first used (Kooshi, 2026-09-06). Each entry records **(i)** what of ours rests on it, **(ii)** where our copy
is, and **(iii)** how much of it has actually been read — by section wherever the read was partial. A theorem in
`CLAUDE.md` § Frontier, or a grokking, that rests on prior art names its entry by the key in column one of § A.*

*This file states current state only. A corrected claim is replaced by the corrected claim, with its
attribution; how each entry was reached — the dated amendments, corrections and struck-out claims — is in git.
The last version carrying that history is commit `f1c78f4` (`git show f1c78f4:BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`).*

*Copies live in `~/source/library/`, outside this repository; copy paths below are relative to `~/source/`.
`library/papers/` holds Kooshi's downloads, `library/pdf/` the search agent's and later fetches,
`library/notes/` text extractions, search findings; the one book sits at the top
level. § B lists every held file with its key and read status.*

*Markers.* **[title from a search listing]** — the title was seen only in web-search results and has not been
checked against the paper. **[title unverified]** (or **[unverified]**) — the description is ours, not the paper's. Rows marked
*(analysis ✓)* or *(analysis ~)* came from the web-Claude session's analysis (`reference/Chat-analysis/`,
2026-09-06), carrying that session's confidence mark, and are second-hand here unless the row says otherwise.
Read status: **read in full**; **read** with the parts named; **scanned** (abstract, introduction, results and
reference list, or the parts named); **abstract**; **second-hand** (content known through another work, which the
row names); **not read**. **Held** means a copy is in `~/source/library/`.

## E. Glossary — this project's vocabulary against the literature's

prior art**: a working term coined here appears in no paper, so a search in our words filters out the literature

found `[Vinh2006]` used *the discipline's* phrase, not ours.

literature, but we should replace any where the scope is small and our chosen term might lead to confusion."**

replacement where the term is both narrow and confusable, and closed milestone records left alone** — a
