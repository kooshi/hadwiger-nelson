### 1.2 The results, in my words; what each rests on; what the library holds

Notation (the library's): `F : Subfield ℝ` with `[NumberField F]` is a real number field; `L = F(i)` is `fieldI F`, a
subfield of `ℂ`; `σ` is complex conjugation on `L` (`conjOn (fieldI_conj F)`), `conjO F` on `𝓞 L`. A *stable prime* is a
maximal ideal of `𝓞 L` fixed by `σ` (`SpectralChain.StablePrime F`); it is *ramified* when `σ` acts trivially on its residue
field (`Rigidity.IsRamified`), and a ramified stable prime is dyadic (`IsRamified.isDyadic`), so the scope
`NoRamifiedStableDyadic F` says that no stable prime is ramified (`noRamifiedStableDyadic_iff`). `R = ℤ[U_F]` is the subring of
`L` generated by the norm-one elements, `UnitRing.unitRing (conjOn (fieldI_conj F))` — the unit vectors of `F²` read in `L`;
the same ring as a subring of `ℂ` is `UnitRing.unitRingOver F` (`= SpectralChain.unitRing F`), the two related by
`UnitRing.coe_mem_unitRingOver_iff`. `O′ = stableIntegers F` is the intersection of the localizations `P.localRing` over the
stable primes `P`.

**Lemma C′** (row 100's peel; FS91-J07-4 = J10-T6). *If no stable prime of `F(i)` is ramified, the ring generated by the unit
vectors is exactly the ring of elements of `F(i)` that are integral at every stable prime.* The derivation's own statement is
wider and per prime: `R̄_p = ∏_{stable 𝔓 | p} Ô_𝔓` for every `p`, "except possibly `p = 2` when some stable dyadic place is
ramified"; the typed head statement is its global corollary. Rests on: by hand, three derivations (J09's J2, J07-4,
J10-T6). **What the library holds** (all on the standard three axioms):

- `Rigidity.unitRing_le_stableIntegers` — `R ⊆ O′` (every real number field).
