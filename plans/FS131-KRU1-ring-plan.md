### 1.2 The results, in my words; what each rests on; what the library holds

Notation (the library's): `F : Subfield ℝ` with `[NumberField F]` is a real number field; `L = F(i)` is `fieldI F`, a
subfield of `ℂ`; `σ` is complex conjugation on `L` (`conjOn (fieldI_conj F)`), `conjO F` on `𝓞 L`. A *stable prime* is a
maximal ideal of `𝓞 L` fixed by `σ` (`SpectralChain.StablePrime F`); it is *ramified* when `σ` acts trivially on its residue
field (`Rigidity.IsRamified`), and a ramified stable prime is dyadic (`IsRamified.isDyadic`), so the scope
`NoRamifiedStableDyadic F` says that no stable prime is ramified (`noRamifiedStableDyadic_iff`). `R = ℤ[U_F]` is the subring of
`L` generated by the norm-one elements, `UnitRing.unitRing (conjOn (fieldI_conj F))` — the unit vectors of `F²` read in `L`;
the same ring as a subring of `ℂ` is `UnitRing.unitRingOver F` (`= SpectralChain.unitRing F`), the two related by
`UnitRing.coe_mem_unitRingOver_iff`. `O′ = stableIntegers F` is the intersection of the localizations `P.localRing` over the
stable primes `P`.

**Lemma C′** (row 100's peel; FS91-J07-4 = J10-T6). *If no stable prime of `F(i)` is ramified, the ring generated by the unit
vectors is exactly the ring of elements of `F(i)` that are integral at every stable prime.* The derivation's own statement is
wider and per prime: `R̄_p = ∏_{stable 𝔓 | p} Ô_𝔓` for every `p`, "except possibly `p = 2` when some stable dyadic place is
ramified"; the typed head statement is its global corollary. Rests on: by hand, three derivations (J09's J2, J07-4,
J10-T6). **What the library holds** (all on the standard three axioms):

- `Rigidity.unitRing_le_stableIntegers` — `R ⊆ O′` (every real number field).
- `Rigidity.exists_mul_stableIntegers_le` — `c·O′ ⊆ R` for some natural `c ≠ 0` (every real number field, no scope).
- `Rigidity.Counting.exists_normOneL_residue`, `exists_normOneL_residues` (`Rigidity/CountResidue`, FS131-ICL2) — **local
  Hilbert 90 modulo `P^m`, and jointly at finitely many unramified stable primes by the Chinese remainder theorem in `𝓞 L`**:
  every family of norm-one residues `s_P ∈ 𝓞 L ⧸ P^m` (`P ∈ Φ`, each unramified) is the family of residues of one unit
  vector.
- `Rigidity.Counting.exists_mem_nsmul_of_resR_eq_zero` — "Lemma C (d)": residues zero at finitely many stable primes at the
  exponent `m = |𝓞 L ⧸ (M)|` put an element of `R` in `N·R`; its proof holds, inline, the step "`x ∈ 𝓞_P` with residue `0`
  at `P^m` and `M ∉ P^m` gives `x/M ∈ 𝓞_P`" (the valuation-ring dichotomy, no valuation computed).
- `UpperHalf.localRing_ne_top`, `UpperHalf.residue_*`, `UpperHalf.isUnit_mk_pow` — the localization and its residue maps.

What is missing: that the residues of `R` at finitely many unramified stable primes fill the whole product ring

  "every real number field has a stable prime", and the previous planner's "Chebotarev-type fact" is the whole clause.

  prime** — and the prime of `F(i)` above it is stable (`UpperHalf.exists_stablePrime_card_eq_sq`, FS131-ICS1's bridge,
  proved on its branch). So every real number field has a usable prime and a stable prime, and its plane has infinitely

### 2.4 Route E: a stable prime at every real number field, no outside theorem (the ruled route)

[hand], one mind (the coordinator checked the congruence step by hand, item 1040). `θ` an integral primitive element of
`F ⊆ ℝ` (AdmissiblePrime's `exists_integral_primitive`), `f = minpoly ℤ θ`, monic, `n = deg f`, `θ` a real simple root
(`f′(θ) ≠ 0` by separability, as in `exists_dvd_of_dvd_exponent`). `f < 0` on some `(a, b)` (sign change at a simple root).
`D = 4·M·E·|disc F|`, `E` from `exists_dvd_of_dvd_exponent`. Choose `e ≥ 1`, `t` with `x/k ∈ (a, b)`, `k = Dᵉ`, `x = 1 + Dt`.
`N := −(f.scaleRoots k)(x) = −kⁿ f(x/k) > 0`; `D ∣ k` and `f` monic give `(f.scaleRoots k)(x) ≡ xⁿ ≡ 1 (mod D)`, so
`N ≡ −1 (mod D)`: `N % 4 = 3`, and `N` is prime to `D`. A prime `q ∣ N`, `q ≡ 3 (mod 4)`; `q ∤ D` so `q ∤ M`, `q ∤ E`, `q ∤ k`;
in `ℤ/q`, `f(x·k⁻¹) = 0`, so `q ∣ f(t′)` for an integer `t′`. `nonempty_residueHom` (Kummer–Dedekind at `q ∤ exponent θ`)
gives `φ : 𝓞 F →+* ℤ/q`; `𝔭 = ker φ` is maximal with `q` residues, `q ≡ 3 (mod 4)`: usable, and `q ∈ 𝔭`.
**The stable prime above** — two routes, both short:
- *if FS131-ICS1 has landed* (`Arith/StableAboveUsable` on `main`): `nonempty_stablePrime` is one line from
