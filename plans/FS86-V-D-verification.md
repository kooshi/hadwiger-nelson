### VD-T1 — **PROVED, both directions; the smooth case suffices** [hand; mine; the citation checked first-hand]

*Setting.* `k₀ = ℚ(√3) ⊂ ℚ₂(√3) =: 𝒦`, dense. A configuration of `n` points `p = (p_1, …, p_n) ∈ 𝒦^{2n}` with `p_1 = 0`, `p_2 = (4, 0)`,
induced unit-distance graph `G` (triangle-free), `(p_1, p_2)` forced at four colours in `G`. Forcing and triangle-freeness are properties
of the abstract finite graph with its two named vertices.

*⟸.* A field embedding `E ↪ 𝒦` preserves `(x−x′)² + (y−y′)² = 1` and `≠ 1` both ways, so a configuration in `E²` is one in `𝒦²` with the
**same** induced graph. ∎ (trivial, as predicted)

*⟹.* Let `X ⊂ 𝔸^{2n}_ℚ` be the edge equations of `G` plus `z_1 = 0, z_2 = (4, 0)`; the non-edges (`≠ 1`) and distinctness are open conditions
`O` holding at `p`. Let `𝔮 = ker(k₀[z] → 𝒦, z ↦ p)` and `Z = V(𝔮) ⊆ X_{k₀}`: an integral `k₀`-variety of dimension `d`, of which `p` is the
**generic point** (every non-zero regular function on `Z` is non-zero at `p`). In characteristic 0, `Z` is smooth over `k₀` on a non-empty
open set, which therefore contains `p`. **So every `𝒦`-point is a smooth point of its own `k₀`-Zariski closure** — this is the whole
reduction of the general case to the smooth one. Jacobian criterion: pick generators `g_1, …, g_{2n−d}` of `𝔮` and coordinates `J`
(`|J| = 2n − d`) with `det(∂g/∂z_J)(p) ≠ 0`; near `p`, `V(g)` and `Z` agree on a Zariski (hence 2-adically) open neighbourhood `N` of `p`
(`V(g)` is regular of dimension `d` at `p`, `Z ⊆ V(g)` has dimension `d` there). The 2-adic analytic implicit function theorem gives
`z_J = φ(z_I)` on a neighbourhood of `p_I`, `I` the other `d` coordinates. Choose `c ∈ k₀^d` close to `p_I` (density) and put
`p′ = (c, φ(c)) ∈ Z(𝒦)`. The `k₀`-scheme `Y = Z ∩ {z_I = c}` has zero tangent space at `p′` (the `z_J`-Jacobian is invertible), so
`dim_{p′} Y_𝒦 = 0`; if the scheme point `y ∈ Y` under `p′` were not closed, `{y}‾` would have positive dimension `e` and `Y_𝒦 ⊇ ({y}‾)_𝒦`
would have dimension `e` at `p′` — so `y` is closed, `κ(y)` is a number field, and `p′` has coordinates in the number field
`E = k₀(p′) ⊂ 𝒦`. For `c` close enough, `p′` satisfies `O`; it satisfies `X`'s equations because `Z ⊆ X`. So `p′ ⊂ E²` realises `G`
exactly, with `p′_1 = 0`, `p′_2 = (4, 0)`: triangle-free and forcing `(0, 4)`. ∎

*The citation, found and read first-hand* (web search for a literature statement, not a finite-witness search). **F.-V. Kuhlmann, *The
algebra and model theory of tame valued fields*, J. reine angew. Math. 719 (2016); arXiv:1304.0194v2, Theorem 5.9 and Corollary 5.10**,
verbatim: *"Theorem 5.9. Let (K, v) be a henselian field. Assume that (L|K, v) is a separable subextension of (K^c|K, v). Then (K, v)
is existentially closed in (L, v)."* and *"Corollary 5.10. A henselian field (K, v) is existentially closed in its completion K^c if and
only if the extension K^c|K is separable."* Applied with `K` = the algebraic closure of ℚ in `𝒦` (henselian: a simple root mod `𝔪` lifts
in `𝒦` and is algebraic, so lies in `K`; dense, since `K ⊇ k₀`; completion `𝒦`; separable, characteristic 0), it gives ⟹ at once:
the existential sentence (edges, `≠ 1` via `w·(f − 1) = 1`, distinctness likewise, the two fixed points) true in `𝒦` is true in `K`,
and the finitely many coordinates generate a number field. The classical DVR form is Greenberg (Publ. IHÉS 31, 1966), not read here.
Copy: `fs86-v-d/kuhlmann-tame-1304.0194.pdf` (scratch; not in `~/source/library/`, not in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` — a debt if the result is filed).
**Verdict on the brief's question: the smooth case suffices** — by the generic-point reduction above, which uses only generic smoothness
in characteristic 0, the Jacobian criterion, the 2-adic implicit function theorem and the density of `ℚ(√3)` in `𝒦`. The citation is
correct and applicable but not needed.

*Adversarial attempt, by formula.* A configuration realisable only at singular points of `X` — e.g. a rigid degenerate framework whose
realisation space over `𝒦` is a single singular point. Then `Z` is that point's closure: the point is Zariski-closed over `k₀`, so it is
already algebraic (`d = 0`, nothing to approximate). A positive-dimensional `Z` whose `𝒦`-points are all singular is impossible, since `p`
itself is the generic point. Fails.

#### (2) The restricted theorem under H\* — PROVED [hand; mine; inputs: B1's Lemma R (density, generalised verbatim) and Lemma P, Lemma D + FS38-D2-B (every minimiser of every `P_m` kills `𝔓⁴`), weak approximation for the rational norm-one torus]

*Weights.* Enumerate the places `v₁, v₂, …` of `E` (all stable, split and archimedean) and let `S_n` be the first `n`. Local probability
measures `ν_v^{(n)}` on `T(E_v)`, each symmetric under `t ↦ −t` (so every local transform below is real):

  **iff** `χ_𝔓` is a minimiser, which kills `𝔓⁴` (Lemma D; D2-B). Other stable places: `> −1/3` (H\*), and `= 1` iff `χ_v = 1` (the circle
  spans the local ring).
- split `v` (`L_v = E_v × E_v`, `T(E_v) = {(t, t⁻¹)}`): half uniform on `π^{−k_n}O_v^×`, half on `π^{k_n}O_v^×`, `k_n → ∞`. For
  `a_v = (a′, a″)` and `n` large the transform is exactly `½[a′ = 0] + ½[a″ = 0]` (the far term is in `ker ψ`; `∫_{O^×} ψ(bs) ds = 0` once

Weak approximation (the norm-one torus is rational) makes `U` dense in `∏_{v∈S_n} T(E_v)`, so a finitely supported `w_n` on `U` can be
chosen with every atom in a prescribed small cell (a congruence class at finite places, an `ε_n`-ball at `∞`), approximating

*Limits.* For `χ = χ_a ∈ 𝒳` with its canonical components on the finite set `S(χ)`, `χ(u) = ∏_{v∈S(χ)} ψ_v(a_v u)` for `u ∈ U`

`λ_{w_n}(χ) := Σ_u w_n(u) Re χ(u) → Λ(χ) := ∏_{v∈S(χ)} Λ_v(χ_v)`. Every factor lies in `[−1/3, 1]`, and all but the dyadic one lie in `(−1/3, 1]`.

negative factor; and a factor in `(−1/3, 1)` makes the size of the product `< 1/3`.) So equality holds iff `χ = χ_𝔓 ∘ π_𝔓` with `χ_𝔓` a
minimiser.

`g(0) = g(y)` a.s. for each of countably many `y`. So `c` is a.s. `(R ∩ 𝔓⁴)`-periodic, a proper colouring of `R/(R ∩ 𝔓⁴) = O/𝔓⁴`
(onto by density), and hence a `P₄`-pullback (every edge of `P₄` lifts). ∎
