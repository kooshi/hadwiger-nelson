## What FS-91 proved, in plain words

A **band character** at `k` colours of a real number field `F` is a group homomorphism `θ` from the ring `R_F = ℤ[U_F]` (generated by the
unit vectors of the plane `F²`, read as complex numbers in `F(i)`) to the circle `ℝ/ℤ` that sends every unit vector into the band
`[1/k, 1 − 1/k]`; then `x ↦ ⌊kθ(x)⌋` is a proper `k`-colouring of the plane — a **character colouring**. Every residue target of `F` (the
unit-distance Cayley graph of a residue ring `O_𝔓/𝔓^m` at a conjugation-stable prime `𝔓` of `F(i)`) pulls its band characters back to band
characters of `F`.

FS-91 proved (`plans/FS91-derivation.md` §1, §4; `LEDGER.md`'s FS-91 rows, numbered by J24):
- **Band rigidity at every band** (FS91-J01-7, two blind readings): every band character is a torsion character plus an archimedean one.
- **The class theorem CLG, at existence scope** (FS91-J07-7, two independent derivations): "`Θ_k(R_F) ≠ ∅` iff some stable place `𝔓` of
  `F(i)` and some level `m ≥ 1` have a band `k`-character of `T_{𝔓,m}`. Hence `χ_char(F²) = min_{𝔓,m} χ_char(T_{𝔓,m})`" (verbatim,
  `plans/FS91-S1D-character.md` §3.1). The proof peels: a sum of symmetric subsets of the circle inside the band keeps **some** summand inside
  it (FS91-J07-2) — never "one" (J07 §5.1 (iv)).
- **CLG-g** (FS91-J07-11): given `H_k`, `Θ_k(R_F) ≠ ∅` implies `L(F) ≤ k`, "unless every stable place carrying a band `k`-character carries
  it only above level one and is (E1) odd with residue field `𝔽_{p^f}`, `f ≥ 3`, `2p < k < χ(Γ_{p^f})`, or (E2) inert dyadic of norm
  `Q ≥ 64` with `k < χ(U_Q)` and `k ≥` `U_Q`'s Hoffman floor". No exception at `k ≤ 5`, none in degree ≤ 2. Its corollary (g3) at `k = 7`
  holds only in the corrected form "exact level `2e(𝔭|3) + 1`" — a GAP in J07's lemmas found by the blind verifier (J10-T8).

**What CLG does not say** (the owner's scope note at FS-91's close, `plans/FS91-close-inventory.md` item 163): CLG is an existence
statement. It does **not** say that every character colouring of the plane is pulled back from one stable place. That stronger
classification is proved at three colours (FS91-J09-J4, "no mixing": with no ramified stable dyadic prime, `Θ₃(R_F)` is exactly the `4N₃`
residue characters at the primes of residue field `𝔽₃`; two independent derivations) and at four colours over ℚ(√59), ℚ(√83), ℚ(√131)
(HD1, from CLG's *proof* with Lemma D and tightness, FS91-J07-12) — and nowhere else.

## The questions

1. **The classification form (the structural question the milestone's §IQ asked and answered with "some").** For `k ≥ 4` and a real number
   field `F`: is every `θ ∈ Θ_k(R_F)` the pullback of a band `k`-character of one residue target `T_{𝔓,m}` plus an archimedean part? If not,
   what is the other summands' freedom — which targets carry a non-zero character that can ride on a band summand's margin?
   *A starting observation (the close planner's, one mind, unverified; for the reframe to check):* in the peel, the summands other than the
   band one must keep the sum in the band, so their values on the unit vectors lie within the band summand's margin of 0; on a level-one
   target `Γ_q` a character's value set is `V_{N(a)}/q`, about `(q+1)/2` points (FS91-J08-1), which cannot fit in a short interval about 0
   — so the question may reduce to margins against value-set sizes at every level, with the dyadic and archimedean places treated apart.
2. **CLG-g's exception.** Is (E1) or (E2) ever realised — a band 6- or 7-character above level one of the `U₆₄` tower (fields of degree ≥ 6
   with an inert dyadic place of norm 64), or at exact level `2e(𝔭|3) + 1` above 3 with residue field `𝔽₂₇` (fields of degree ≥ 3)?
3. **(g3) at `e ≥ 2`.** Does a further lemma kill exact level `2e + 1` at `e(𝔭|3) ≥ 2` (J07's form), or is the corrected form sharp?
   J10's scope data: LB′ is sharp at (`e = 1`, level 2) and (`e = 2`, level 3) on `f = 1` towers, not at (1, 3) or (2, 5), where the true
   best margin is `1/27` (`plans/FS91-S1V-character.md` §3.2).

## Why it matters, stated honestly

**Bearing on χ ≥ 6: none directly.** It decides the *shape* a character falsifier of the local–global conjecture would need: CLG-g already
excludes character colourings with fewer than `L(F)` colours at `k ≤ 5` and over quadratic fields; the exception is the only character
route left at six and seven, and the classification says whether a character colouring can be local at no prime at all.
