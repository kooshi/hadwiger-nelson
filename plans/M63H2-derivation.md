**M63H2-T1 — The one-integer family: a rational-integer diagonal radius forces exactly `cos θ = (2n−1)/(2n)`, and nothing else. [hand; the product owner, 2026-09-12; the family's n=16 member, Poros, PH2-4]** For a type-M rotation `ρ = cos θ + i·sin θ`, the **diagonal circle** — points at unit distance from their own rotated image — has squared radius `r² = 1/(2 − 2cos θ)`. Then **`r²` is a rational integer `n` if and only if `cos θ = (2n−1)/(2n)`**. *Proof:* write `cos θ = c/d` in lowest terms; `r² = d/(2(d−c))`; `r² = n` gives `d = 2n(d−c)`, so `c = d(2n−1)/(2n)`, and `gcd(c,d)=1` forces `d = 2n`, `c = 2n−1`. Confirmed independently by exhaustive search over every reduced `c/d` with `d < 400`. `plans/M63-phaseH2-the-family.md` §3. *Proof verdict:* closes. *Rests on it:* the whole four-gate screen (M63H2-T2–T4); ledger row 64; `CLAUDE.md` G7's family theorem; FS-22 (the `ℚ(√3,√7,√11)` stub, raised by this identification). *Prior art:* the family itself — `cos θ = (2m²−1)/(2m²)` at `n = m²` — is the `m₁=m₂=m, r=1` slice of `[PartsOdd2022]` §3's law-of-cosines formula `ρ(r) = exp(i·arccos((m₁²+m₂²−r²)/(2m₁m₂)))`, and `[Polymath16wikiMain]` (2018) states the same family directly as `ω_t = exp(i·arccos(1−1/(2t)))`, attaching the spindle reading in words and listing `ω₁₆ = ρ₆₃` as one of the four generators of de Grey's own ring. **Neither source states the if-and-only-if** — that a rational-integer diagonal radius forces exactly this family and nothing wider — and that is ours, one step from a formula both sources already write down. **M63H2-T7 — The counting compulsion (`CLAUDE.md` G53): a base-alone floor that can refute a type-M arena. [derived; the product owner, from a solver-agent measurement, 2026-09-12]** For a type-M arena (base `B` plus a rotated copy sharing the centre `0`, joined by the diagonal cross edges of the free circle) and any colour count `k`: a proper `k`-colouring `C` of the arena gives two proper `k`-colourings of `B` alone, `f(v)=C(v)` and `g(v)=C(Rv)` — `R0=0` gives `f(0)=g(0)`, and each diagonal cross edge `(v,Rv)` gives `f(v)≠g(v)`. With `B(h) = \{$circle $v : h(v)=h(0)\}$}`, any `v ∈ B(f)∩B(g)` would give `f(v)=f(0)=g(0)=g(v)`, contradicting the second fact — so `B(f)` and `B(g)` are disjoint. Hence, with `L = min` over proper `k`-colourings of `B` of `|B(h)|`: **`2L > (\text{circle count}) ⟹ $the arena is not $k$-colourable}`**, a property of the base alone, no arena solve. *Proof:* `plans/M63-phaseH2-the-family.md` §6e, three steps. Uses only the diagonal cross edges (off-diagonal ones can only help the refutation); sufficient, not necessary. **Instantiated on `ρ₆₃`'s base at `k=4`:** `L=10` exactly (M63H2-T10's exhaustive enumeration: `≤9` UNSAT, `≤10` SAT, two-sided), circle count 30, so `2L=20 ≤ 30` — the criterion is **silent** on `ρ₆₃`; it does not itself refute, and `ρ₆₃`'s non-4-colourability rests on the separate argument of M63H2-T11. *Proof verdict:* closes (the general theorem is sound; its non-firing on `ρ₆₃` is a correctly reported negative result, not a gap). *Rests on it:* ledger row 64's mention of the family; `CLAUDE.md` G53 in full. **Not a theorem, demoted here:** the earlier reading "exactly 10 of 30, every time" from twelve *reseeded* colourings — retracted in the same source once pattern-blocking (which forbids, rather than merely re-asks for, a different colouring) exhibited a colouring with all 30 circle points at the centre's colour; the "provisional 10/30" is a floor (`L ≥ 10`) established by a flawed sampling method, not an invariant, and is superseded by M63H2-T10's exact `L=10`. *Prior art:* ours; the shape (a lower-bound "colour-class floor" argument from an isometric pair of colourings glued at one shared point) has no located citation; nearest held: none specific; zbMATH: `ab:"unit distance graph" & ab:"chromatic number" & ab:"rotated copy"` (0 hits), `ab:"coincidence site lattice" & ab:"coloring"` (0 hits). *Kernel:* declined — a short counting argument over `Fin k`-colourings of a finite graph; a natural small Lean statement (any `k`, any type-M arena) if the construction is ever formalised, worth a stub beside `Forcer.lean`.
