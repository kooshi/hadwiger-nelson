*The theorem: χ(G(K²)) = 4 for K = ℚ(√3, √11) — every finite unit-distance graph with all coordinates
in K is properly 4-colourable (`~/field-wall-conjecture.md` §5, Theorem B; the record in `CLAUDE.md`
ledger row 47 says "hand-proved, NOT kernel-checked"). This stub prices the formalization and fixes
its shape so that a fresh session can subplan it. Numbered when minted.*

## 1. What is to be proved, in the repository's currency

`unitDistance ℂ` is the standing graph. The statement:

    theorem four_colorable_of_seam_field (S : Finset ℂ) (hS : ∀ z ∈ S, z.re ∈ K ∧ z.im ∈ K) :
        ((unitDistance ℂ).induce (S : Set ℂ)).Colorable 4

with `K : IntermediateField ℚ ℝ := ℚ⟮√3, √11⟯` (or the concrete carrier of `Field/Quadratic.lean`
restricted to its √3, √11 leaves — see §4). Companion: `four_le_chromaticNumber` is already held by
the Moser-spindle instance (`Instances/…`), so equality is one `le_antisymm` away once the spindle's
coordinates are exhibited in K (they are: 0, ζ₆, ζ₃, i√3, ρζ₆, ρζ₃, ρ·i√3, all with coordinates in the
basis 1, √3, √11, √33 — the note's §4).

## 2. The proof, in the elementary shape that avoids the ring of integers

The note's proof runs through O_L and a prime above 2. The ring of integers of L = ℚ(i, √3, √11) is
NOT needed and is inconvenient (the obvious order has index 9). The shape that formalizes:

1. **The order.** R := ℤ[X, Y]/(Φ₁₂(X), Y² − Y − 8), i.e. R = ℤ[ζ₁₂, T] with T = (1 + √33)/2. It is a
   free ℤ-module of rank 8 with basis ζ₁₂^a T^b (a < 4, b < 2), an integral domain, and its fraction
   field is L (ζ₁₂ = (√3 + i)/2 gives √3 and i; T gives √33 and hence √11 = √33/√3). Concretely R is
   realised as a subring of ℂ: `Algebra.adjoin ℤ {ζ₁₂, T}`; injectivity of the presentation is the
   ℚ-linear independence of {1, √3, √11, √33} ⊗ {1, i} — `Ladder.sqrt_indep` already holds the real
   half.
2. **The residue map.** φ : R → 𝔽₄ = GF(2)[ω]/(ω² + ω + 1), X ↦ ω², Y ↦ 0. Well-defined because
   Φ₁₂(ω²) = ω² + ω + 1 = 0 and 0² − 0 − 8 ≡ 0 in characteristic 2. Its kernel P is a maximal ideal
   with R/P ≅ 𝔽₄, and **P = (1 − i, T)R** (R/(1 − i, T) ≅ 𝔽₂[X]/(X² + X + 1): with i = 1 the relation
   Φ₁₂ becomes X² − X − 1 ≡ X² + X + 1 after 2 = 0, and 2 = 0 follows from 2X = 0 and X² = X + 1).
3. **Conjugation preserves P.** Complex conjugation c maps R to R (c(ζ₁₂) = ζ₁₂¹¹, c(T) = T) and
   φ ∘ c = Frob ∘ φ on generators (ζ₁₂¹¹ ↦ ω²² = ω = (ω²)², T ↦ 0), hence c(P) = P. This replaces the
   decomposition-group argument entirely.
4. **The local ring is a valuation ring.** A := R_P (Localization.AtPrime). It is a Noetherian local
   domain (R is a finitely generated ℤ-algebra, hence Noetherian), not a field, and its maximal ideal
   PA is PRINCIPAL, generated by π = 1 − i: T = (1 − i)² · (4i/(T − 1)) with T − 1 ∉ P (φ(T − 1) = 1),
   so T ∈ π²A. By the DVR characterisation (`IsDiscreteValuationRing.TFAE` — a Noetherian local domain
   with principal maximal ideal is a DVR, hence a valuation ring) A is a valuation ring of L: for every
   z ∈ L, z ∈ A or z⁻¹ ∈ A.
5. **Unit vectors are units of A.** For u ∈ L with u · c(u) = 1: if u ∈ A then c(u) = u⁻¹ ∈ c(A) = A
   (c(R) = R and c(P) = P give c(A) = A), so u ∈ A×; if u ∉ A then u⁻¹ = c(u) ∈ A, so u = c(c(u)) ∈
   c(A) = A, contradiction. No valuation function is ever named.
6. **The colouring.** φ extends to A → 𝔽₄ (localization at P: φ(p/q) = φ(p)/φ(q)); a unit of A has
   nonzero residue; for a finite S ⊂ L pick a base point per coset of the additive group A and colour
   z ↦ φ(z − z₀). Two points at unit distance differ by a unit vector u ∈ A×, so their residues differ.
   That is `Colorable 4` with `Fin 4 ≃ 𝔽₄`.

Every step is either finite algebra (1–3, 6) or one Mathlib theorem (4: the TFAE; the Noetherian
instance for a finitely generated ℤ-algebra). The residue field 𝔽₄ can be `GaloisField 2 2` or a
hand-rolled four-element ring — hand-rolled is cheaper for `decide`.

## 4. Two design choices to settle in the subplan

* **Carrier of K.** Either `IntermediateField ℚ ℝ` generated by √3, √11 (mathematically clean, all
  facts by `linear_combination`), or the repository's computable tower (`Field/Quadratic.lean`'s
  `Adjoin` restricted to two leaves) with `eval` into ℝ. The theorem quantifies over ALL K-points, so
  no kernel computation on coordinates is needed anywhere; the IntermediateField route is right, and
  the tower's `eval` gives the bridge for any concrete instance (the Moser spindle, the E–I seed).
* **Fin 4 versus 𝔽₄.** `Colorable 4` wants `Fin 4`; colour by an explicit bijection 𝔽₄ ≃ Fin 4.

## 5. Price

The proof is ~a page of mathematics; the formalization is dominated by plumbing: the presentation
of R and its injectivity (the largest item — ~200 lines with the independence lemma), the DVR
characterisation's hypotheses (~150 lines), φ and conjugation (~100), the colouring (~80). Estimate
600–900 lines, two to four sittings, no compute. Risks: the exact Mathlib names for the TFAE and
for Noetherianity of finitely generated ℤ-algebras (verify by `exact?`/grep before writing a line);
`AdjoinRoot` over ℤ being awkward (fallback: define R as `Algebra.adjoin ℤ {ζ₁₂, T} : Subalgebra ℤ ℂ`
and prove φ exists via the universal property of the polynomial ring plus the kernel computation).

## 6. Spec examples the module must carry

* the Moser spindle's seven K-points, coloured by φ, proper on its eleven edges (decide-scale once
  the residues are literals);
* the four residues of ζ₆, ρ, σ, τ₅ as in the note's §5.3 table (ω², ω, ω², ω²);
* a planted red: a point at distance 2 from the origin gets residue 0 = the origin's — the
  colouring is NOT proper on distance-2 pairs, and a version of the theorem for the {1, 2}-graph would
  be false (Exoo–Ismailescu).

## 7. Why it earns a milestone

It is the programme's first plane-colouring theorem over a field beyond ℚ, the certificate behind
the halt of M53, and — by its §6 generalisation — a statement about every real number field. The
publication-bar policy is unchanged (nothing below χ ≥ 6 is written up); the kernel receipt is for
the repository's own standard: a hand proof that stopped a campaign should not stay hand-proved.

**The headline.**

```lean
theorem four_colorable_of_seam_field : ((unitDistance ℂ).induce seamPlane).Colorable 4
theorem colorable_four_of_subset_seamPlane (S : Set ℂ) (hS : S ⊆ seamPlane) :
    ((unitDistance ℂ).induce S).Colorable 4
theorem chromaticNumber_seamPlane : ((unitDistance ℂ).induce seamPlane).chromaticNumber = 4
```

Stated for the **whole plane over `K`**, not just finite graphs: the coset colouring needs no
finiteness, so §1's `Finset` form is the two-line corollary rather than the theorem. `seamField` is
a `Subfield ℝ` (the ℚ-span of `1, √3, √11, √33`, with `inv_mem` proved), so the vertex set really is
`K²` and the name is earned. Also exported, because they are what the Frontier actually cites:
`not_forces_five_of_subset_seamPlane` and `not_separated_five_of_subset_seamPlane` — the sentence
that halted M53, now a theorem — and `residueColouring_eq_of_dist_two`, the reason no version of
Theorem B can hold for the `{1, 2}`-distance graph.

**§2's route was not taken, and did not need to be.** The stub priced `Localization.AtPrime`,
`IsDiscreteValuationRing.TFAE` and `ValuationRing`, and named `AdjoinRoot`-over-ℤ as the risk. None
of it appears. In the basis `{1, ω} × {1, i} × {1, T}` the joint kernel of the two residue maps is
*visibly* `(1+i)·O`: with `z = p + q·i`, the two conditions say `p ≡ q (mod 2)` in all four
coordinates and then `z/(1+i) = (p+q)/2 + ((q−p)/2)·i` is integral — three small lemmas
(`exists_mul_onePlusI`), no ideal, no localization, no valuation. Conjugation's step 3 becomes one
equality of ring homomorphisms, `psi_comp_conj : (psi s).comp conj = F4.sq.comp (psi s)`, checked on
two generators through a universal property (`Quad.hom_ext`) that the three-layer structure has for
free. **The stop rules never fired**; `Subfield` (design choice D5, the medium-confidence one) landed
in ~90 lines through two quadratic conjugate steps rather than one quartic identity.

**The unexpected simplification.** `ζ₆ = 1 + ω`, `ζ₃ = ω`, `i√3 = 1 + 2ω` and `9ρ = 7 − ω + T + 2ωT`,
so all seven Moser-spindle points are `den x / 9` for explicit `x : SeamOrder` — and since `den` is a
ring homomorphism intertwining the two conjugations, **each of the eleven unit distances is the single
identity `(xᵢ − xⱼ) · conj (xᵢ − xⱼ) = 81` in the order**, decided by the kernel over all forty-nine
pairs at once. §8 contains no `Real.sqrt` manipulation at all.

**Two corrections the kernel forced.** The subplan's first product table for `seamSpan` had
`√11·√33 = 3√3`; it is `11√3`, and the `√3`-coefficient of a product is `11(cd′ + dc′)`. And the
write-up's §5.3 residue table (`ζ₆ ↦ ω², ρ ↦ ω, σ ↦ ω², τ₅ ↦ ω²`) disagrees with this file's
(`Ω², Ω², Ω², Ω`) because they are **the two different primes**: fixing `√33 ↦ s ≡ 17 (mod 256)` makes
`T ≡ 1`, so the note's prime is `psiOne`, not `psiZero`. Both colour; the module docstring records it
so a reader comparing tables does not conclude one is wrong.

**§6's spec examples all ship**, with each order element pinned to its complex value before its
residue is read (`den_rho9`, `den_sigma3`, `den_tauFive25`), plus the spindle's seven residues (all
four colours used, eleven edges distinct), a worked `K²`-membership example through the `Subfield`
API, and the negative controls. **Mutation audit** (registered in the subplan §7): dropping `Odd m`
from `psiOf_eq`, weakening the headline to `Colorable 3`, and collapsing `psiOne` to `psiZero` each
