### 1.3 What reading EL2003 changed before any C0 was written (disclosed, since it colours C0)

EL2003 p. 102, verbatim: *"In this announcement we give an essentially complete proof of Theorem 5.1 regarding the relationship between
entropies of individual elements of an irreducible action. … A sketch of how Theorem 1.1 is proved using Theorem 5.1 is given in §6. Full
details of the proofs of all theorems announced in this note will be given in [5]."* ([5] = "Rigidity properties of measure preserving
Zd-actions on tori and solenoids, in preparation" — which became EL2021; EL2021 §1.4: *"we never seemed to have the time to finally write
down the general case of the results announced in [7]"*.) EL2003 §6 is titled *"Outline of the proof of Theorem 1.1"* and ends: *"Using the
total irreducibility of α it is not hard to conclude at this stage that µ is Haar measure on X."* Its Prop. 5.3: *"We give no details
here"*. **So EL2003 is not a proof of its Thm 1.1; EL2021's "already proven in the announcement [7]" overstates it.** Route (i) of the
plan's §PA.1 is closed; (a0) rests on route (ii), Thm 1.3 plus a deduction.
