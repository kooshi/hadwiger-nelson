## Survey

**The goal, in the principal's words.** Kooshi: beat 509, and *"it's a rehearsal for the real hunt"*
(FS-6, χ ≥ 6). The programme (`plans/PROGRAMME-ALGEBRAIC-PROOF.md` §1, §6) says the derivation
outranks the object and *"an object is finished when its derivation is."*

**Frame on the five axes, each with where it lives.**

| axis | M62 as built | address |
|---|---|---|
| object | a vertex subset of a 10,802-vertex arena (symmetric core of a ball), fixed rotation `ρ₁₅` | `M62 §R7`; `typem.py:426`, `:349` |
| question | *which subset is smallest and still refuses four colours* | `M62 §1` phases C–D |
| discipline | combinatorial geometry + our algebra | `M62 §PA3` |
| audience | this repository | — |
| product | a 509-beating object, as a rehearsal for FS-6 | `M62` header |

**The gap.** The milestone's stated advantage (`§PA3`, `§M2.1`) is that our theorems act as a *search
principle*. Measured: the sieve's 412 admissible rotations are written to
`data/m62/rotations-r2.05.json` and **no code reads them** — `grep` over `tools/` finds one consumer,
a `print` at `typem.py:305`; the arena is hard-wired to `ρ₁₅` (`typem.py:349`, `:464`), the rotation
every published construction already uses. The derivation half and the search half are disconnected,
and the connector that would join them — the pattern decomposition — is wired as a drop filter.

## Recommendation, in four parts

**Choice.** Stop the descent, run card 1 (minutes), and let its answer choose between repairing the
acceptance test and moving the milestone's search object from *vertices* to *pattern sets*.

**Alternative weighed.** Let the running descent finish on the theory that 5,191 is still falling.
It buys a number for the record and costs a day.

**Why this one wins against it.** The descent's acceptance test is a fixed point that the record's
own L may not satisfy; if so the day produces a measurement of the test. Card 1 decides that for the
cost of one enumeration on a 375-vertex graph, and this project's own G21 says to point a new
instrument at a known positive before believing anything it reports.

**Confidence.** High on the diagnosis, medium on the direction of the answer. The one fact that
would change it: the enumeration in card 1 returning exactly 55.

**Default if nothing is done.** The descent continues from 5,191 toward a fixed point set by the
full arena's pattern set, at 128 s per UNSAT on an object ten times the size the record-holder
states as his method's limit, with 412 derived rotations unread on disk.
