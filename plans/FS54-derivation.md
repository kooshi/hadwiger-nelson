**FS54-T3 — The structure of `UD(𝔽_{3^f},2)` for `f` odd, general in `f`. [hand; the FS-54 implementer, 2026-09-16, tested at f=3]** For every odd `f` and `q = 3^f`: (i) every edge lies in exactly one triangle, and that triangle is the
`𝔽₃`-line `{z, z+u, z+2u}` through its unit direction (in characteristic 3, `2u = −u`, so a line's three points are
pairwise unit-distant; a third point `w` off the edge needs `Tr(w) = 1`, whose solutions are the roots of the doubled
polynomial `X² − X + 1 = (X+1)²`, giving exactly one point); (ii) the spectrum is integral (every Cayley graph on an
elementary abelian 3-group has eigenvalues in `ℤ[ζ₃] ∩ ℝ = ℤ`, `[BridgesMena1982]`), with `λ_a = 3m_a − (q+1)/2`

sum); hence every eigenvalue is `≡ 1 (mod 3)`. **At `q = 27`** this forbids `λ = −11` (Weil's bound `|λ| ≤ 2√27 = 10.39`
already excludes anything below `−10`, and the congruence excludes `−8 < λ < −5`… the next value down from `−8`), so
`λ_min ≥ −8`, attained (56 of the 364 hyperplanes hold exactly two directions); `|Aut(UD(𝔽₂₇,2))| = 122{,}472 = 729·56·3`

**FS54-T5 — `W_f`: the fractional wall, with no rounding, and its CRT-product closure. [proof, GPT Astra, 2026-09-16, verified here by recomputation]** For a finite vertex-transitive graph `G` on `n` vertices,
`χ_f(G) = n/α(G)` (direct proof: summing the covering constraints over vertices gives `n ≤ α · weight`; conversely
translating a maximum independent set through the vertex-transitive group and weighting each of the `n` translates `1/α`
gives a fractional colouring of cost `n/α`). Hence `α(U_Q) < Q²/6` proves `χ_f(U_Q) > 6`, hence `χ(U_Q) ≥ 7`, **with no
rounding assumption** (the weaker, previously-used reading `χ = ⌈n/α⌉` is a separate, unproved assertion). By Zhang's theorem (vertex-transitive `G, H`: `α(G×H)/|G×H| = max(α(G)/|G|, α(H)/|H|)`, `[Zhang2012]` Thm 1.8)
and Zhu's fractional Hedetniemi theorem (`χ_f(G×H) = min(χ_f(G), χ_f(H))` for all finite graphs, `[Zhu2011]`, restated as
`[Zhu2025]` Thm 2.4), for a categorical product of norm-one Cayley graphs `χ_f(Π U_{Q_i}) = min_i χ_f(U_{Q_i})`: **if every
factor satisfies `α(U_Q) < Q²/6`, the product's independence ratio is also below `1/6` and the product cannot be
six-coloured** — closing the "composite ideal" escape hatch with no integral form of Hedetniemi needed. *Proof:*
`reference/wall-at-six/responses/2026-09-16-poros-fractional-wall.md` "The connection and its consequence" (lines 27–80),
the direct `χ_f = n/α` proof and the product application in full; `plans/FS54-twenty-seven.md` §R rows R1.a, R1.b (the
verdict table checking each claim, "correct" both times, against the papers read in full). *Proof verdict:* closes
(re-derived here: the `χ_f = n/α` direct argument is elementary and checks; Zhang's and Zhu's theorems are cited from

**FS54-T6 — `U₃₂` is completely rigid at four colours: every maximum independent set is an `𝔽₄`-hyperplane, 44 in total. [proof, GPT Astra, 2026-09-16, verified here by recomputation]** Let
`E = 𝔽_{1024}`, `S = μ_{33}` (the norm-one elements), `U_{32} = Cay(E,+,S)`. (a) **A char-2 Sidon property**: for
`u, v ∈ S` with `u+v=d≠0`, `d̄ = d/(uv)`, so `d` determines the unordered pair `{u,v}` as the roots of `X²−dX+d/d̄=0`; a
nonzero displacement has at most one such pair in odd characteristic away from antipodal sums, and (specialising to `Q=2^m`)
exactly `2` or `0` common neighbours according to `Tr_{𝔽_Q/𝔽_2}(1/N(d))`. (b) **An explicit colouring**: with `β` a
primitive 11th root, `S = 𝔽₄^*⟨β⟩`, and `T(x) = Tr_{E/𝔽₄}(x)`, the map `x ↦ T(x)` is a proper 4-colouring with all classes of
size 256, so `α(U₃₂) = 256`, `χ(U₃₂) = 4`. (c) **The least eigenspace is exactly `S`**: the nontrivial eigenvalues lie in
`{−11,−7,−3,1,5,9}` (Weil), every multiplicity a multiple of 33; the spectral moments (computed combinatorially, without a
spectral solve, from closed-walk counts and the Sidon property) force, via the polynomial `P(X)=(X+7)(X+3)(X−5)(X−9)`,
`10r_{−11}+r_1=15` with `r_{−11}` a positive integer, so `r_{−11}=1`: `{a≠0 : λ_a=−11} = S` exactly. (d) **Rigidity**: for
any maximum independent set `A` (`|A|=256`), Hoffman-tightness plus (c) forces `supp(f̂) ⊆ {0}∪S`; the Sidon property forces
the support `B` to satisfy `|B|≤3` and, when nonempty, `B={a,b,a+b}` with the Fourier coefficients pinned to `±1/4`; the
sum-product identity of (a) then forces `b/a ∈ 𝔽₄^*`, so **`A` is an `𝔽₄`-hyperplane** `{x : Tr_{E/𝔽₄}(ax)=c}`. There are
`33/3=11` directions and 4 translates each: **exactly 44 maximum independent sets, and 11 unordered colour partitions**.

**FS54-T7 — Inert towers keep `λ_min/d`: `λ_min(G_j) = Q^{j−1}·λ_min(G₁)`. [proof, GPT Astra, 2026-09-16, verified here by recomputation; partly prior art]** Let `O` be a discrete valuation ring with uniformiser `π`
and finite residue field of order `Q` (`Q` even or `Q ≡ 3 (mod 4)`), `O_E` its unramified quadratic extension, `A_j =
O_E/π^jO_E`, `S_j` the norm-one elements, `G_j = Cay(A_j,+,S_j)`. Then for every `j ≥ 1`: `|A_j| = Q^{2j}`,
`d_j = Q^{j−1}(Q+1)`, and `λ_min(G_j) = Q^{j−1}·λ_min(G₁)` — so `1 + d_j/|λ_min(G_j)| = 1 + (Q+1)/|λ_min(U_Q)|`, the Hoffman
floor is exactly unchanged at every level. *The argument:* the norm-one conic is smooth over the residue field (a nonzero polar
derivative at every unit), giving `Q` lifts per point and the degree formula. Characters trivial on the last layer factor
through `A_{j−1}` and inherit eigenvalue `Q·(previous)`; a character nontrivial on the last layer sums to zero over each
"tangent line" coset of the last layer unless the character's linear functional vanishes on that tangent, which happens
for at most one (odd characteristic) or exactly one (characteristic two) residue point per functional, so such characters
have eigenvalue at most `2Q^{j−1}` (odd) or `Q^{j−1}` (even) in absolute value — below `Q^{j−1}λ_min(G₁)` in every case
since `λ_min(G₁) ≤ −2` (odd, a 4-cycle) or `≤ −1` (even, an edge). Induction on inherited characters and attainment at a
character inherited from `G₁`'s own least eigenspace closes the theorem. *Proof:*

independently, not merely re-read: `tools/fs54_wall_feedback_verify.py tower` at `q = 3` (levels 1–4), `7` (1–3), `11, 19`,
`23` (1–2), **`27` over the Galois ring `GR(9,3)`** (level 2: `λ_min = −216 = 27·(−8)`, matching), and `Q=2` over
`ℤ₂[ω]` (levels 1–6). *Proof verdict:* closes (recomputation confirms the formula at every tested level and ring, odd and

**FS54-T9 — The symmetric-search reduction is complete: prime-order stabiliser classes suffice. [hand; the FS-54 implementer, 2026-09-16]** For a finite graph `G` with automorphism group `Aut(G)` a finite solvable group:

and, restricted to prime order, up to the finitely many conjugacy classes GAP enumerates. *Proof:* `plans/FS54-twenty-seven.md`

**FS54-T10 — The period-obstruction bound at `U₆₄`: a dimension-≥8-periodic independent set has ≤ 512 points. [hand; the FS-54 implementer, 2026-09-16; independently reproved by GPT Astra as a control]** If

**FS54-T11 — The Fourier-energy stability bound at `U₆₄`: a six-colour class must concentrate on the least eigenspace. [hand + tools; the FS-54 implementer, 2026-09-16]** A 683-point independent set of `U₆₄` (the size a six-colour class must
