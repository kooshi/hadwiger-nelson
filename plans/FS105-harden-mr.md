## §IQ — the invariant question for EF_F⁺ (WORKFLOW 3.3)

1. **Existence form.** At ℚ(√635087), is there a `U`-invariant finite measure on `R̂`, not carried by `T + A`, with zero entropy for one
   non-torsion `u`?
2. **Structural form.** For a countable abelian group `Σ` of automorphisms of a compact abelian group whose elements of height `≤ M` number
   at least `M^δ`, what does **every** `Σ`-invariant measure with zero entropy for one element carry, place by place? (The arithmetic kept
   only as "polynomially many multipliers of bounded height" and "a product formula".)
3. **The invariant it forces.** Named: entropy against growth — at the expanding place of the zero-entropy element, the number of thin
   boxes a set of large measure needs grows subexponentially, while the multipliers grow polynomially; the product formula turns a
   pigeonholed coincidence into an exact global relation (EF2010's Lemmas 3.1–3.2 on 𝕋). The blind job names it in its own words.
4. **Route.** Derivation only; exact arithmetic of seconds may check counts (e.g. `#U_ℚ ∩ U_M`).
5. **The open instances it would decide.** MR at row 100's exception fields (ℚ(√5), ℚ(√17), ℚ(α) — the adelic model there); MR at a
   field with a complex place (EF-G (a) false there, row 120); de Grey's field (claim (c)); not the finitely generated case (Laca–Warren's).
