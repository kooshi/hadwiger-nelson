## The question nobody has asked

A 6-forcer needs an Act One over 5-colourings: a region where **no proper
5-colouring avoids some pattern class everywhere**. Every prior k = 5
measurement in this project (and, to our knowledge, anywhere) was an
instance meter — forced pairs, virtual edges, coverage of a single circle —
and M33 proved instance meters are structurally blind to class compulsions
(the d2 pigeonhole sat under M32's flat instance meters). The
class-avoidance instrument transfers to five colours with one flag. If ANY
class fires at k = 5, it is the first concrete piece of a χ ≥ 6 argument
anyone has held.
