## The mission, in plain language

The rational four-space `ℚ⁴`, with points joined at distance 1, needs exactly four colours (Benda–Perles; this project's R57-T1): the Hurwitz
quaternions reduced at their one two-sided prime above 2, `H/𝔓 ≅ 𝔽₄`, four-colour it. That is the same structure as the plane over ℚ(√59) —
one tight dyadic place with residue field `𝔽₄` — **with triangles** (four rational points pairwise at distance 1 exist). Row 88 asks, over
ℚ(√59), whether every four-colouring is the dyadic pullback. The same question over `ℚ⁴` is a control this project had not named: if every
four-colouring of `G(ℚ⁴)` is a pullback, the triangle-full analogue holds and row 88's difficulty is the missing triangles (G57, G70); if not,
the dyadic structure alone does not force rigidity.

**The one printed structural statement about every four-colouring of a rational unit-distance graph** is Zaks 1989 (*On four-colourings of the
rational four-space*, Aequationes Math. 37, 259–266; Zbl 0705.05033), whose reviewer writes that "in every 4-colouring of `G(ℚ⁴)` every colour is
everywhere dense" — J28's transcription (J35 §6.3 transcribes it with `G(ℚ^p)`); read from the review only; the paper is not held.

## The questions

1. Is every proper four-colouring of `G(ℚ⁴)` (per coset of the unit-vector ring) `γ ∘ (reduction at 𝔓)` for a four-colouring `γ` of the residue
   target? And is the forced group at four colours non-zero? (The two are equivalent over ℚ(√59) by row 88's frame, V2–V4; over
   `ℚ⁴` the frame is not established — the non-commutative ring is the first thing to check.)
2. Does row 111's split into invariant measures and transience, and row 112's count (with `m₁` of `ℝ⁴`'s unit-distance graph in place of `ℝ²`'s),
   transfer to `ℚ⁴`? Is the non-tight part's ratio below 1/4 there?
3. Does a triangle-full local forcer exist inside `ℚ⁴` — the analogue of row 90's `4 ∈ F_tf` question, which over ℚ(√59) must be answered without
   triangles?
