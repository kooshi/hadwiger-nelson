## The theorems

**FS50-T1 — The triple-level certificate's soundness. [hand; the FS-50 phase D′ implementer]** Fix a prime `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`,
write `V = 𝔽_p²`, `n = p²`. For an independent set `S ⊆ V` of size `s`, let `Y_S[u,v] = (1/s)·#{z ∈ S : z+u, z+v ∈ S}` (the
normalised triple count) and `M_S[u,v] = (1/s)·|S ∩ (S−(v−u))|` (the pair autocorrelation). Averaging `Y_S`, `M_S` over the
group `Γ` generated by the point stabiliser `O(2,p)` (order `2(p+1)`), the swap `(u,v)↦(v,u)` and the relabelling
`(u,v)↦(−u,v−u)` gives `Γ`-invariant matrices `Ȳ = Y(ȳ)`, `M̄ = M(ȳ)` linear in a vector `ȳ` indexed by `Γ`-orbits of ordered
pairs, satisfying: `y_{k₀}=1` at the origin-pair orbit; `y_k=0` on every orbit touching a unit vector; `y_k ≥ 0`; `y_k ≤ y_{d(k)}`
(`d(k)` the class orbit of `(0,u)`); `Y(ȳ) ⪰ 0`, `M(ȳ)−Y(ȳ) ⪰ 0`; `trace Y(ȳ) = s`. **Theorem:** given rational `λ_i ≥ 0` (one
per inequality row), `ν_j` of either sign (one per equality row), and rational PSD matrices `Z₁, Z₂`, such that
`N − trace Y(y) ≡ ⟨Z₁,Y(y)⟩ + ⟨Z₂,M(y)−Y(y)⟩ + Σ_i λ_i c_i(y) + Σ_j ν_j e_j(y)` identically as an affine function of the orbit
variables `y`, every independent set `S` has `|S| ≤ N`, hence `α(UD(𝔽_p,2)) ≤ ⌊N⌋`. *Proof:* `plans/FS50-certificate-soundness.md`
§§1–3 (the ten facts P1–P10 of the triple-counting matrices; §2's averaging, which shows every fact survives conjugation by
permutation matrices and that `Γ`-invariance makes the pair/triple counts orbit-functions; §3's four-line soundness argument —
every term on the identity's right side is `≥ 0` at `y = ȳ_S`, so `N − s ≥ 0`). *Proof verdict:* closes. *Rests on it:* row 68
(`α(Γ₈₃) ≤ 1046`, hence `χ(Γ₈₃) ≥ 7` on the checker's word); the consistency instances at 11 and 23 (row 53); every certificate
in `data/fs50/`, `data/fs53/`, `data/fs54/`; FS-52. *Prior art:* partly — the triple-level relaxation over an association scheme
is `[Schrijver2005]`'s (§2 of that paper; held **listing only**, recalled by the FS-50 planner and the advisor, not fetched —
`BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`), and the block-diagonalised hierarchy it sits in is `[GvozdenovicLaurent2008]`'s (also listing only,
recalled); FS-50's own prior-art gate (`plans/FS50-prior-art.md` §2) found no exact value of `α`, and no bound below the ratio
bound, for any finite Euclidean plane, under any name searched (finite-Euclidean-graph independence number, generalized Paley
graph, Delsarte–Schrijver LP, Erdős–Falconer single-distance). **The one-page transplant of Schrijver's identity to the Cayley
graph on `𝔽_p²` with its point stabiliser, written so a stranger who has not read Schrijver can check it by exact linear
algebra, is ours** — `[Schrijver2005]` itself is unread here, so the exact correspondence between its general statement and
this specific derivation is unverified; recorded as a check owed. zbMATH: `ab:"unit distance" & ab:"independence number" &
ab:"finite field"` (0 hits, live 2026-09-23), `ti:"Schrijver" & ab:"Terwilliger" & ab:"association scheme"` (not re-queried
here — `[Schrijver2005]`'s own listing-only status is the open item). *Kernel:* stub (FS-52, `plans/STUB-triple-certificate-kernel.md`).

**FS50-T2 — Any rational PSD pair gives a sound certificate for some N (the rounding is never unsound). [hand; the FS-50
phase D′ implementer]** Given ANY rational positive-semidefinite `Z₁, Z₂`, the per-orbit recursion of
`plans/FS50-certificate-soundness.md` §5 — for a non-class orbit `k`, `λ^{le_diag}_k = max(0,−a_k)` with `a_k` the "leftover"
coefficient after subtracting `Z₁`'s and `Z₂`'s contributions at `k`; for a class orbit `k ≠ k₀`, the same rule against the
partial sum `T_k` of `le_diag` multipliers already fixed pointing at `k`; at `k₀`, `N := T_{k₀} − a_{k₀}`; on a killed orbit
the equality multiplier is free, `= a_k` — determines nonnegative `λ_i`, arbitrary-sign `ν_j` and a rational `N` making
FS50-T1's identity hold exactly, coefficient by coefficient. So a wrongly-chosen or rounded `(Z₁,Z₂)` can only make `N` larger
(the certificate weaker); it can never make the certificate unsound. *Proof:* `plans/FS50-certificate-soundness.md` §5 (every
step a maximum against zero, so the construction never fails); confirmed operationally by `data/fs50/README.md`'s "theorem
behind the control" note (`T^T A T ⪰ 0` for any rational `T`, applied to a planted wrong reflection phase and a planted wrong
circle, both of which raised `N` rather than breaking soundness) and by the corrected claim there — "a wrong phase must make N
LARGER is not a theorem" (only the underlying *optimum* is monotone; the rounded `N` carries a solver-noise term of order
`10⁻⁴…10⁻⁶` that is not itself monotone, and one planted-fault run at `p=11` gave a smaller `N` (29.176805 against the faithful
29.176808) while remaining CERTIFIED — the corrected, sound statement is about the optimum, not the rounded `N`, and is
recorded as such). *Proof verdict:* closes. *Rests on it:* the whole producer/checker separation — `tools/fplane_cert_round.py`
is explicitly untrusted (any heuristic, any float solver) because nothing it emits is believed until FS50-T1's checker accepts
it; the circulant/block reformations (FS50-T3, FS50-T4) reuse exactly this fact to justify not verifying the block maps.
*Prior art:* ours — the general shape (Farkas-type duality: any dual-feasible point certifies *some* bound) is LP/SDP folklore,
but the specific closed-form per-orbit recursion for this scheme's constraint system, and its use to separate "rounding
quality" from "soundness", is not cited from a paper anywhere in the milestone's record. *Kernel:* with FS50-T1 (FS-52).

**FS50-T3 — The circulant/Fourier block reformation, sound at any prime. [hand; the FS-50 phase E implementer]** For
`p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, presenting `Z₁, Z₂` as `Z = Σ_b U_b Z_b U_b^T`, where each block `b` corresponds to a Fourier index
`j = 0 … m/2` (`m = p+1`, the rotation group's order) and `U_b`'s columns are cosine/sine weight vectors over the `m` angles
of each quadrance circle, reduces the affine identity's per-orbit sums — which naively cost `O(n²)` — to `O(m)` correlation
sums `A_{xy}[d] = Σ_t x[t]y[t+d]` per block per ordered pair of circles, because summing `U Z_b U^T` over a rotation orbit
slides both weight vectors together. **The reduction's soundness needs no verification of the `U_b`'s correctness**: `Z` is
positive semidefinite for ANY choice of `U_b` (it is `Σ_b U_b Z_b U_b^T` with each `Z_b ⪰ 0`), so a wrong or approximate block
map can only weaken the identity (via FS50-T2), never make it unsound — this is what lets the certificate exist at `p = 83`
(6,889 points, `Z₁,Z₂` each `47`-million-entry matrices that fit in no file) at a cost of about `10⁸` integer operations
instead of `n²` ones. *Proof:* `plans/FS50-certificate-soundness.md` §7 (the presentation, the orbit-sum identity for a
rotation orbit, and the "not on the trust surface" argument); `tools/fplane_sdp_blocks.py`'s docstring (the same reduction,
independently restated as code: `Y[u_cζ^θ, u_{c'}ζ^{θ'}] = f_{cc'}(θ'−θ)`, blocks `B_j[c,c'] = Σ_θ f_{cc'}(θ)ω^{jθ}`). The
orbit-numbering control (`--self-test` at `p = 3,7,11,19,23`: the scalable rotation-orbit construction agrees with the direct
`O(p²)` union-find on every representative, killed flag and trace weight) and the assembled-matrix control (a small circulant
certificate's orbit sums, read off the correlations, equal those read off the fully assembled matrix, which is checked PSD by
an exact `LDL^T` the circulant path never performs) are both reported green. *Proof verdict:* closes. *Rests on it:* the
`p = 83` certificate itself (row 68, `data/fs50/p83-refusal/`); FS-52's kernel target, which would need this same reduction
inside the kernel's cost model. *Prior art:* check owed — `[GvozdenovicLaurent2008]`'s block-diagonalised hierarchy is
recalled, not read, and plausibly contains the general form of this reduction for a symmetric association scheme; the specific
instantiation for `O(2,p) × swap × S₃` on the norm-class scheme of `𝔽_p²`, and the "block map need not be verified" argument
stated as a soundness theorem in its own right, are not found under any query run here. *Kernel:* with FS50-T1 (FS-52) — "the
block-diagonalisation's correctness is never on the trust surface" is itself the reason a kernel proof would not need to
re-derive the reduction, only re-check `T^T A T ⪰ 0`.

**FS50-T4 — Reflection symmetry halves the block size, losslessly (the real block form). [hand + tools; the FS-50 phase E
implementer]** With the base point of each quadrance circle fixed on a common reflection axis (one per circle type), the
angular profile of a `Γ`-invariant matrix satisfies `f_{cc'}(d) = f_{cc'}(a_{c'}−a_c−d)` (`a_c` the reflected base point's
angle), which makes the phase `exp(iπj(a_c−a_{c'})/m)·B_j[c,c']` real: the Hermitian Fourier block of size `C = p−1` (which
the certificate's wire format carries as a real embedding of size `2C`, i.e. twice its true spectrum) is a diagonal-unitary
conjugate of a real symmetric block of size `C`. **The reduction is exactly lossless**: the wire (Hermitian, size `2C`)
block's whole spectrum equals the real (size `C`) block's, each eigenvalue doubled — an equality, not an inequality — so
solving with the small block loses nothing, and the reduced dual is pushed back onto the unreduced wire form by an explicit
congruence `Z_j = T_j W_j T_j^T` before the checker (which is byte-identical throughout this reduction) ever sees it. Any
wrong reflection phase can only weaken the certificate (`T^T A T ⪰ 0` again, FS50-T2's fact), never make it unsound.
*Proof:* `data/fs50/README.md` lines 195–234 ("The real block form" section): the algebraic identity
`⟨W, T_j^T(U_j^T Y U_j) T_j⟩ = ⟨T_j W T_j^T, U_j^T Y U_j⟩` (identically in `Y`); four numerical controls at `p = 11, 23, 83`
— (1) the vectorised wire arrays equal `block_setup`'s map (skipped at 83 by cost, reason printed); (2) the reduced map
equals `T^T A T` to `3.7·10⁻¹³` at 83; (3) **the spectrum-doubling equality**, to `2.4·10⁻¹¹` at 83 — "the one that matters
and it is an equality, not an inequality"; (4) a planted wrong reflection angle and a flipped phase sign both LOSE check (3)
by 107.6 and 575.0 at 83, confirming the control is reachable. Measured payoff: the solve at `p = 23` falls from 882.9 s to
146.9 s at the producer's tolerance (a genuine `6.0×`, distinguished in the record from an unrelated `38×` measured at a
different tolerance on a different tool — one thing differing, per `CLAUDE.md`'s rule). *Proof verdict:* closes (verified
numerically to floating precision at three primes, with reachable negative controls). *Rests on it:* the practicality of the
`p = 83` solve (0.20 s/iteration on GPU against otherwise; row 68's certificate). *Prior art:* ours — the fact that reflection
symmetry makes a Fourier block real is standard representation theory (a diagonal-unitary conjugation of a real form), but the
specific spectrum-doubling identity, its numerical verification protocol, and its use to halve an SDP block size in this
scheme, are not cited from a paper. zbMATH not separately queried (an implementation-level refinement of FS50-T3, not a
distinct mathematical claim). *Kernel:* with FS50-T1/T3 (FS-52).

**FS50-T5 — The commutant's block sizes, by dihedral character theory. [hand; the STUB-independence-threshold.md §M1.3
implementer, cross-checked by the advisor (Poros)]** For `p ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, the permutation representation of `O(2,p)`
(dihedral of order `2(p+1)`) on `V = {0} ∪ (p−1)` circles has character `fix(id) = p²`, `fix(rotation) = 1`,
`fix(reflection) = p`; decomposing gives the trivial representation with multiplicity `p` (one per circle plus the origin),
the two sign representations (rotation acting by `−1`) each with multiplicity `(p−1)/2` (one per circle of the matching
reflection type), and each of the `(p+1)/2 − 1` two-dimensional irreducibles with multiplicity `p−1`. Hence the commutant
of a `Γ`-invariant matrix — and so each of the certificate's PSD blocks at `p = 83` — decomposes into **one `83×83`, two
`41×41` and forty-one `82×82` blocks**. Check at `p = 11`: `11² + 5² + 5² + 5·10² = 671`, the independently-computed Burnside
orbit count on ordered pairs. *Proof:* `plans/STUB-independence-threshold.md` §M1.3 (the character computation and the
`p=11` check); `plans/POROS-2026-09-14-fs50-planning.md` "Q1" (the same computation, run independently by the advisor with
the block sizes `671/3439/6095/285935` orbit variables at `p=11/19/23/83`, and a formula slip caught and corrected —
`(p+1)/2 − 1` multiplicity, not `(p+1)/2`). *Proof verdict:* closes (two independent derivations agreeing, one catching the
other's arithmetic slip). *Rests on it:* FS50-T3, FS50-T4 (the practical block sizes used in the certificate at `p = 83`).
*Prior art:* ours — standard dihedral-group representation theory, not cited from a specific source; `[GvozdenovicLaurent2008]`
(listing only) likely states the general form for an association scheme's automorphism group, but this is not checked.
*Kernel:* declined — representation-theoretic scaffolding behind a numerical pipeline; no stub currently plans a kernel form
of the character computation itself, as distinct from FS-52's use of the resulting blocks.

**FS50-T6 — Consequence: L(K(√7)) = 7. [derived from rows 53, 68]** `K(√7) = ℚ(√3,√7,√11)`'s usable primes (row 53's sieve)
are exactly 83 and every prime `≥ 103`; FS50-T1's certificate gives `χ(Γ₈₃) ≥ 7` (row 68), and every usable prime `≥ 103` has
Kloosterman/Hoffman spectral floor `≥ 8` (row 53, G44's Weil argument). So every usable residue plane of `K(√7)` has
`χ ≥ 7`, hence its local bound `L(K(√7)) = 7`; under the local–global conjecture's bold form (G48), `χ(K(√7)²) = 7`, and
since `K(√7)` is a subfield of `ℝ`, `χ(ℝ²) = 7` follows *under the conjecture* — the mission's target statement, pinned to the
one 6-open field this project has itself built a five-chromatic object in (row 64). *Proof:* `LEDGER.md` row 68's own text
("the sieve's six leaves K(√7) … so L(K(√7)) = 7 and the conjecture predicts χ(K(√7)²) = 7, hence χ(ℝ²) = 7 (G48)"), which is
a direct combination of row 53 (the sieve) and row 68 (FS50-T1's new floor) — nothing else is used. *Proof verdict:* closes
(a one-step combination of two already-held, independently-established facts; the "under the conjecture" qualifier is load-
bearing and is carried in the statement, not dropped). *Rests on it:* § The mission's "target, as of 2026-09-17: seven"; FS-40
(closed with this consequence, per `plans/STUB-independence-threshold.md` §Close: "FS-40 absorbed"). *Prior art:* ours (the

## Readings and measurements that were called theorems, and instruments

- **`ϑ = ϑ′ = Hoffman` on every plane checked (11, 19, 23, 83), so only the triple level sees below the ratio bound.**
  [measured, with a reading]. The mechanism is `[Schrijver1979]`'s theorem — for a graph whose edges are a union of classes
  of a symmetric association scheme, `ϑ` is computed by Delsarte's LP (`ϑ = ϑ′`) — combined with `[Lovasz1979]` Thm 9
  (edge-transitivity gives `ϑ = ` Hoffman), already row 56's citation. What FS-50 adds is the *measurement* that this
  plane's norm-class scheme is such an association scheme and that the equality holds numerically at four primes
  (`tools/fplane.py theta --below 84`) — a confirmation of a known mechanism, not a new theorem. `plans/POROS-2026-09-14-fs50-planning.md`
  §Q2 explicitly corrects an earlier looser phrasing ("'every relaxation is blind' written as a theorem…it is a theorem about
  pair schemes and a measurement about local rows").
- **The autocorrelation LP transplant (the real-plane 1-avoiding-set LP, ported to `𝔽_p²`).** [derived, partly prior art].
  `plans/FS50-prior-art.md` §3: the three lemmas behind `[KMOR2016]` Lemma 3.2(2), Székely's inclusion–exclusion, and
  `[AmbrusEtAl2023]` Lemma 1's atoms hold verbatim on `𝔽_p²` (the finite field's translation-invariance and averaging over
  `O(2,p)` play the role the real plane's continuous symmetry plays), giving an LP strictly between Delsarte's and the triple
  level. Measured to bite 12% at `p=11` and 0.2% at `p=83` (against the 4.2% the threshold needed) — an instrument, not
  filed as a standalone theorem because its content is "the mechanism (theirs) transplants (ours), and the transplant's
  measured bite is what decided the certificate needed a stronger instrument" (§S2 of `plans/STUB-independence-threshold.md`).
  This is what motivated the escalation to FS50-T1's triple level.
- **The rounding mechanism: "round from a fresh continuation's own dual, never a long-run iterate."** [method]. Recorded in
  `LEDGER.md` row 68 and `data/fs50/README.md`; a discipline for using FS50-T1/T2 correctly (an SDP solver's adaptive scale
  is not checkpointed, so a long-run iterate's rounding can land far from the true optimum — measured 200 points off at one
  point), not itself a mathematical theorem.
- **The block sizes and orbit counts at 11/19/23/83 (671/3,439/6,095/285,935 before S₃; ~111/573/1,015/47,655 after).**
  [tools]. A checked enumeration, the numerical input to FS50-T5, not a separate theorem.
