   coset a pullback, `X₄(F) = Pull` — or is there a four-colouring that is not a pullback? χ(ℚ(√59)²) = 4 is known (rows 74, 82, 83 below;
   the dyadic place is inert in `L/F` with `U₄ = K₄`), so the question is rigidity at the top rung, not the chromatic number.

   pullback in every archimedean direction, patches no two pullbacks, is 2-adically discontinuous, is no measurable window over finitely
   many places and **no character** (rows 91, 92, 96; HD1).
3. **What the crunch added** (index `plans/CRUNCH-2026-09-25-INDEX.md`): M1's CLG, an existence statement — *"`Θ_k(R_F) ≠ ∅` if and only if
   some stable place `𝔓` of `F(i)` and some level `m ≥ 1` have a band `k`-character of `T_{𝔓,m}`"* (`plans/FS91-S1D-character.md` §3.1) —

   finite invariant subset. Row 88's question is **whether Pull is all of it** — G76's closed-set frame, which FS-89 ran on characters
