1. **The question** is row 88's: over a type-(B) field, is `F(R) ≠ 0` — equivalently `F(R) = P(R) = R ∩ 𝔓⁴`, every four-colouring of every
   coset a pullback, `X₄(F) = Pull` — or is there a four-colouring that is not a pullback? χ(ℚ(√59)²) = 4 is known (rows 74, 82, 83 below;
   the dyadic place is inert in `L/F` with `U₄ = K₄`), so the question is rigidity at the top rung, not the chromatic number.
2. **What the record already fixes** (rows 88–96): the frame (`F(R) ∈ {0, P(R)}`, `P(R) = R ∩ 𝔓⁴`); the local plane over `ℚ₂(√3)` rigid,
   so a yes is a realisation question (`4 ∈ F_tf`, row 90) and at least Soifer 55.1 (row 86); a no is a colouring that disagrees with every
   pullback in every archimedean direction, patches no two pullbacks, is 2-adically discontinuous, is no measurable window over finitely
   many places and **no character** (rows 91, 92, 96; HD1).
3. **What the crunch added** (index `plans/CRUNCH-2026-09-25-INDEX.md`): M1's CLG, an existence statement — *"`Θ_k(R_F) ≠ ∅` if and only if
   some stable place `𝔓` of `F(i)` and some level `m ≥ 1` have a band `k`-character of `T_{𝔓,m}`"* (`plans/FS91-S1D-character.md` §3.1) —
   with HD1 re-proved through it with fewer inputs (J07-12, J10-T4); M3's measurable-window machinery, J21-MX (*"a compact factor with
   `λ* < 1/(k−1)` can be deleted from a Borel `k`-colouring of a product"*, index §3.6) and J21-RED — one mind each, unverified.
4. **The reframe's recognition** (§G.4, §IQ): `X₄(F)` is a compact space with an action of the amenable group `Aff_F`, and Pull is a
   finite invariant subset. Row 88's question is **whether Pull is all of it** — G76's closed-set frame, which FS-89 ran on characters
   (`R̂`), lifted to colourings. It splits into two halves, each a named kind of statement: **(Q-μ)** every `Aff_F`-invariant probability
   measure on `X₄(F)` is carried by Pull; **(Q-tr)** no non-pullback is *transient* (has every invariant measure on its orbit closure carried
   by Pull). Rows 91's no-gos (walls, half-space defects, patchworks) are partial answers to Q-tr; rows 92, 94 and 96 to Q-μ.
