**T1 — Lemma 1.1, the local-ring lifting lemma. [hand; GPT Astra]** Let `F ⊂ ℝ` be a number field, `E = F(i)`, `U` its unit vectors (`u ū = 1`), `R = ℤ[U]` the ring they generate.
