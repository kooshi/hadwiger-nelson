### 1.2 The terms, fixed for this record

At a point `p` of a weak colouring three things are in play, and the records use all three under two names.

- **`Γ_p`, the transition graph** (`transitions.tex` Definition, ll. 28–40): the pair `ij` is an edge when in every ball about
  `p` the area of `i`–`j` transitions under a shift `h` is not `o(|h|)`. A property of **every small ball about `p`**. A
  **cyclic centre** is a point whose `Γ_p` has a cycle.
- **`W_p`, the witness graph** (the object of FS132-E5's form (O), `plans/FS132-E5-derivation.md` ll. 509–511): the pair
  `{a, b}` is an edge when some `q ≠ p` has `H_a` and `H_b` both missing `Δ({p, q})`. A property of **a neighbourhood of the
  unit circle about `p`**. A **witnessed cycle** is a cycle of `W_p`.
- **presence**: `a` is present at `p` when `p ∈ H_a`. The weakest ball property; `Γ_p`'s edges need it at both ends
  (`transitions.tex` ll. 44–46).
