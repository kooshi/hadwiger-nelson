### 1.3 Findings from the readback

- **F1 — the definition of `L(F)` (the asking lane asked the planner to fix it).** The kernel's `SpectralChain.localBound F`
  is `⨅ P : StablePrime F, (levelTarget P 1).chromaticNumber`: **level one only**, and its target's connection set is the
  **norm-one residues** `{x : x·x̄ = 1}` of `𝓞_L ⧸ P`, not "the image of the unit vectors". The POROS § 3.3 sentence reads it
  as "every level `m`, the image of the unit vectors" — that is not the kernel's definition (`CLAUDE.md` § Notation agrees
  with the kernel: "`L(F)` reads level one"). The two readings agree through five: FS100-CLS says so ("level-one or
  all-levels reading"), and the kernel's upper half holds at every level (`UpperHalf.chromaticNumber_planeOver_le_levelTarget`),
  so `min(L_all(F), 5) = min(L(F), 5)` is a 15-line corollary of the headline; planned below (`min_localBoundAllLevels_five`).
  The image of `U` equals the norm-one set at every level of an unramified stable prime (local Hilbert 90 + CRT, part B's
  leaf `exists_unit_residue_eq`), and at a ramified one the target is two-colourable either way; not needed by part A.
  **Correction owed** (wording, not truth): the published theorem's sentence should read "`m = 1`, the norm-one residues".
- **F2 — the class and CLS's equivalence are already kernel theorems**, wider than row 123 says: the class needs no
  squarefreeness and works at every real number field holding `√d`. What row 123's "CLS in full" adds is the list, and the
  list's every case is proved privately; the theorem is a re-assembly (§ 2.3).
- **F3 — no bridge from a usable prime of `F` to a stable prime of `F(i)`.** The library states the upper half twice — at a
  usable prime of `F` (`chromaticNumber_planeOver_le_finitePlane`) and at a stable prime of `F(i)`
  (`chromaticNumber_planeOver_le_localBound`) — and no theorem says the first's `Γ_q` is a level-one target of the second. So
  no named field has `L ≤ 5` in the kernel, and "`L = 5`" at the family and at `ℚ(√23, ∛2)` needs that bridge. Planned as a
  general lemma (`localBound_le_unitQuadrance`), which also states row 52's upper half in `L`'s own words.
- **F4 — the complex place asks nothing of the library's notions.** `F : Subfield ℝ` with `[NumberField F]` is a number field
  with one chosen real embedding; its other places, real or complex, are invisible to `StablePrime` (primes of `𝓞 (F(i))`),
  and the headline carries no `IsTotallyReal`. `ℚ(√23, ∛2)` is the subfield of `ℝ` generated by `√23` and the real cube root
  of 2; it is an instance of the headline once `5 ≤ L` is shown. That the instance has a complex place is a statement about
  `F`'s other embeddings; planned as an optional spec leaf (`not_isTotallyReal`), since it is what makes the instance the
  complex-place case.
- **F5 (row 123, second route at ℚ(√167))** — row 123 lists `χ(ℚ(√167)²) ≥ 5` "by two routes"; the kernel holds the class
  route only. Not in this job's scope; noted.

### 2.3 A3 — CLS in full (≈ 260 lines)

Statement shape (the classification as an arithmetic predicate, with the ledger's three kinds):
`IsFourColourPlace P := IsRamified P ∨ ∃ Q ∈ {2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 16, 32}, |𝓞_L ⧸ P| = Q²` — `Q` is the size of `F`'s residue field
when `P` is unramified (`StandingFive.card_fixed_of_involution`), so `Q ∈ {3, 7}` is "residue field `𝔽₃` or `𝔽₇`" and
`Q = 2^f ≤ 32` "dyadic inert of norm ≤ 32"; a ramified stable prime is dyadic (`UpperHalf.two_mem_of_conj_trivial`). Then:
per prime, `(levelTarget P 1).Colorable 4 ↔ IsFourColourPlace P` and `(∃ c < 1/4, RatioAtMost P c) ↔ ¬ IsFourColourPlace P`;
per field, `5 ≤ L(F) ↔ ∀ P, ¬ IsFourColourPlace P` and `TCA F ↔ …`; the all-levels reading `5 ≤ L_all(F) ↔ 5 ≤ L(F)`.
Each case is in `Rigidity/ClassRatio`, three of them private: read them with Batteries' `open private … from
Proofs.HadwigerNelson.Rigidity.ClassRatio` (precedent: `Rigidity/BochnerTau`, `LEAN.md` l. 418), so no existing module is
edited and nothing downstream of `ClassRatio` recompiles. The large-dyadic case (`f ≥ 6` gives ratio `< 1/4` with no
colouring hypothesis) is the second half of private `ratioAtMost_dyadic`, which takes `5 ≤ χ` only to dismiss `f ≤ 5`: the
leaf reproduces its `f ≥ 6` branch over the opened private lemmas (`exists_card_two_pow_sq`, `charBound_one_of_charSum`,
`charSum_64_ge`, `Floor128.charSum_128_ge`, `charSum_dyadic_large`, `ratioAtMost_of_charBound`, `charBound_of_charBound_oneT`).
A cleaner alternative, drafted for Keryx (§ 3.5 item 1), is a `ClassRatio` edit that states the `f ≥ 6` branch as its own
public lemma; it recompiles everything downstream of `ClassRatio` (`Rigidity/ChainHeadline` onward), so the plan does not
take it.

### 2.4 The bridge: a usable prime is a level-one target (≈ 300 lines; general, new)

`localBound_le_unitQuadrance (h𝔭 : IsUsablePrime 𝔭) : localBound F ≤ (unitQuadrance (𝓞 F ⧸ 𝔭)).chromaticNumber`, and its
`finitePlane p` form. Route: (u1) `A_𝔭 := {z ∈ F(i) : re z, im z ∈ V_𝔭}` (`V_𝔭` the valuation subring of `placeHom_base`'s
proof, `Arith.exists_valuationSubring_localization`) is a conjugation-stable valuation subring `≠ ⊤` of `F(i)` — for
`z = a + bi`, say `b = ta`, `t ∈ V_𝔭`: `1 + t²` is a unit (−1 is no square in `𝔽_q`, the `hnon` step of `placeHom_base`),
so `(1 + ti)⁻¹ ∈ A_𝔭`, and `z` or `z⁻¹` lies in `A_𝔭` as `a` or `a⁻¹` lies in `V_𝔭`; then
`UpperHalf.exists_stablePrime_of_valuationSubring` gives `P` with `P.localRing = A_𝔭`. (u2) The fixed field of conjugation
on `𝓞_L ⧸ P` is the image of `V_𝔭`, so it has `q` elements (a fixed `x = res(a + bi)` has `res(2bi) = 0`, `2 ∉ P`).
(u3) The level-one hom to `unitQuadrance k₀`, `k₀` the fixed field — the `φ` of private `chromaticNumber_le_four_of_small`,
which is generic in `Q` — composed with `k₀ ≃+* 𝓞 F ⧸ 𝔭` (`FiniteField.ringEquivOfCardEq`) and `unitQuadrance.map`.

### 2.6 B — Lemma C′ (≈ 700 lines; route FS91-J10's, refined here)

Held: `R ⊆ O′`, `c·O′ ⊆ R`. Leaves:
(b0) `R = O′` iff `O′ ⊆ R + c·O′` (given `c·O′ ⊆ R`) — 15.
(b1) finitely many stable primes contain `c` (`Ideal.finite_factors`-style, or private `finite_stablePrime_absNorm_le`) — 30.
(b2) for `x ∈ O′` and `r ∈ R` agreeing modulo `P^{v_P(c)}` at each of them, `x − r ∈ c·O′` (valuations at `P.heightOne`) — 100.
(b3) local Hilbert 90 at an unramified stable prime: every norm-one `w ∈ 𝓞_L ⧸ P^m` is `z/z̄` with `z ∉ P` — take
`z = a + w·ā` with `a = 1`, or, where `w ≡ −1 (mod P)`, `a` with `ā − a ∉ P` (exists exactly when `¬ IsRamified P`) — 70.
(b4) CRT in `𝓞_L` (`IsDedekindDomain.exists_forall_sub_mem_ideal`, `LEAN.md` l. 385) — so `U` maps onto the product of the
local norm-one groups (weak approximation for the norm-one torus, with no stereographic parametrization) — 60.
(b5) local generation: the norm-one residues generate `𝓞_L ⧸ P^m` as a ring at an unramified stable prime — level one by
counting (a subfield of `𝔽_{Q²}` with more than `Q` elements is everything); level `m + 1` from `m` because the subring's
intersection with `P^m ⧸ P^{m+1}` is an `𝔽_{Q²}`-subspace of a line and holds a nonzero fibre difference (the Hensel count,
private `card_res_fibreT` of `ClassRatio`, opened) — 200.
(b6) a subring of a finite product of local rings holding a product of subgroups with an element `≠ 1 (mod 𝔪_j)` in each
holds the idempotents, so is the product of the generated subrings — 120.
(b7) assembly, model (`ℚ(√47)`: no stable dyadic prime) and spec — 100.
The infinitely-many-components clause (C8): `[L : R] = ∞` given one stable prime (≈ 40 lines). **Its scope "every real
number field" needs a stable prime at every `F`**: a non-split prime of `F(i)/F`, a Chebotarev-type input that Mathlib does
