witnessed — measures each in-context compulsion's cheat support: (86,183) d=3 virtual edge

## Failure clauses and priors (stub-level)

The §Sʹʹʹ-era caveats stand: in-context kill rates never extrapolate context-free (the P4b/G30
quantifier slip); the currency is the separation odds / conditional counts, never raw entropy
(two adversarial members recorded); weight trends are pre-refuted as design signals (the quad's
mass inversion). Prior that a context-free gadget at EITHER nominated distance is reachable by
directed design within one milestone: 0.2 — the honest number; the milestone's guaranteed value
is the gradient curve itself (how forcing decays as cheats are deleted), which no one has ever
measured and which prices the endgame of ANY route to six.
