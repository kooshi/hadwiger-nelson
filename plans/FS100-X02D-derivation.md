## Stage 2 — SAVED (HX-1, PE-1)

**Lemma F (finiteness)** [hand, textbook]. *A torsion-free abelian group `A` of rank `n` has `|A/mA| ≤ mⁿ` for every `m ≥ 1`.* For a prime `p`,
`dim_{𝔽_p} A/pA ≤ n`: lift `n + 1` classes, take a ℤ-relation, divide it by the largest power of `p` dividing every coefficient (legitimate
since `A` is torsion-free), and reduce mod `p`. Multiplication by `pᵏ` maps `A/pA` onto `pᵏA/pᵏ⁺¹A`, so `|A/pᵉA| ≤ p^{en}`; and
`A/mA ≅ ⊕_p A/p^{v_p(m)}A`. ∎ *Consequence used throughout:* if `M ⊂ L` is a finitely generated module over a ring `S ⊂ L` and `N ⊆ M` is an
`S`-submodule with `ℚN = ℚM`, then `M/N` is finite (it is finitely generated over `S` and torsion, so one integer `m ≥ 1` kills it, and it is
a quotient of `M/mM`).

### 2.1 HX-1 — **TRUE** [hand]

1. Pontryagin duality (`R` discrete, `R̂` compact): closed subgroups `Y ⊆ R̂` correspond to subgroups `N = Y^⊥ ⊆ R`, with `Y = N^⊥ ≅ (R/N)^`;
   and `u·Y ⊆ Y` for all `u ∈ U` iff `uN ⊆ N` for all `u ∈ U` (the action is the dual of multiplication, and `U` is a group). [textbook]
2. `U` is a multiplicative group, so `R = ℤ[U]` is the ℤ-span of `U`; a `U`-stable additive subgroup `N` is therefore an **ideal** of `R`.
3. A nonzero ideal `I` has finite index: for `0 ≠ x ∈ I` take its primitive minimal polynomial `c_d x^d + … + c₁x + c₀` over ℤ; `c₀ ≠ 0` and
   `c₀ = −x(c_d x^{d−1} + … + c₁) ∈ xR ⊆ I`, so `R/I` is a quotient of `R/c₀R`, finite by Lemma F (`R ⊂ L` is torsion-free of rank `[L:ℚ]`).
4. So `Y` is finite if `N ≠ 0`, and `Y = R̂` if `N = 0`. ∎

Nothing about `R` beyond "a subring of a number field" is used — not `R = O′` (row 100). **Remark:** HX-1 says `U` acts irreducibly on
`R̂`; the obstruction in row 118 is only that no *finitely generated* `Γ` does.

## Stage 3 — SAVED (PE-2) — **TRUE** [hand, given EL2021 Thm 1.2 (external, in print) and four textbook facts]

### 3.1 Lemma Γ [hand] — the statement the proof derives from (§1.4)

*For admissible `Γ` and a `Γ`-invariant, `Γ`-ergodic probability measure `ρ` on `R̂`: `ρ = m`, or `h_ρ(γ) = 0` for every `γ ∈ Γ`.*

1. `ρ_k := (π_k)_*ρ` is `Γ`-invariant and `Γ`-ergodic (a `Γ`-invariant Borel set of `M̂_k` pulls back to one of `R̂`).
2. By PE-1′ and E1: `ρ_k = m_{M_k}`, or `h_{ρ_k}(γ) = 0` for all `γ ∈ Γ`. (E1's conclusion is "`h_µ(α^n) = 0` for all `n ∈ ℤ^d`", and `α^n` is
   multiplication by the `n`-th element of `Γ`: this **is** "for every `γ ∈ Γ`" — the planner's question on (2), answered.)
3. `𝒜_k := π_k⁻¹(Borel M̂_k)` increase, are `γ`-invariant (`M_k` is `γ^{±1}`-stable), and generate the Borel σ-algebra of `R̂` (it is
   generated by the evaluations `χ ↦ χ(r)`, `r ∈ R`, and `r ∈ M_k` for large `k`). By T2, `h_ρ(γ) = lim_k h_{ρ_k}(γ)`, non-decreasing in `k`.
4. If `h_ρ(γ) > 0` for some `γ ∈ Γ`, then `h_{ρ_k}(γ) > 0` for all `k ≥ k₀`, so `ρ_k = m_{M_k} = (π_k)_*m` for all `k ≥ k₀` (Haar pushes to Haar
   under an onto continuous homomorphism). Each Fourier coefficient `∫ χ(r) dρ(χ)` is computed on `M̂_k` for `k` large, so it equals `m`'s; by
   T4, `ρ = m`. Otherwise `h_ρ(γ) = 0` for all `γ ∈ Γ`. ∎

`U` enters nowhere in Lemma Γ.

### 3.2 From `Γ` to `U`: the advisor's route, written out and checked [hand]

Let `ν` be a `U`-invariant probability measure on `R̂`, `Γ` admissible, and `ν = ∫ ρ dP(ρ)` its `Γ`-ergodic decomposition (T1; `Γ ⊂ U`, so `ν`
is `Γ`-invariant). Write `Erg_Γ` for the `Γ`-ergodic measures and `Z_Γ = {ρ ∈ Erg_Γ ∖ {m} : h_ρ(γ) = 0 ∀γ ∈ Γ}`; by Lemma Γ,
`Erg_Γ = {m} ⊔ Z_Γ` up to the point `m` (which is `Γ`-ergodic: a character `r ∈ R` with finite `Γ`-orbit is fixed by some `γ ≠ 1`, and
`γr = r` forces `r = 0`).

1. **`U` permutes the components.** `U` is abelian, so each `u` commutes with `Γ` on `R̂`; `u_*` maps `Erg_Γ` onto itself, fixes `m`, and
   preserves `Z_Γ` (`u` is a measurable conjugacy from `(R̂, ρ, γ)` to `(R̂, u_*ρ, γ)`, so `h_{u_*ρ}(γ) = h_ρ(γ)`). From `u_*ν = ν` and the
   uniqueness in T1, `(u_*)_♯P = P`.
2. **The split.** `a_Γ := P({m})`, `ν = a_Γ·m + (1 − a_Γ)ν′_Γ` with `ν′_Γ` the normalised integral over `Z_Γ`; `ν′_Γ` is `U`-invariant (it is
   `(ν − a_Γ m)/(1 − a_Γ)`), and `h_{ν′_Γ}(γ) = 0` for all `γ ∈ Γ` by T3.
3. **`U`-ergodic `ν`.** `ν` is extreme among `U`-invariant probability measures, so `a_Γ ∈ {0, 1}`: if `0 < a_Γ < 1`, extremality gives
   `ν′_Γ = m`, but `m` is `Γ`-ergodic, so its representing measure is `δ_m`, while `ν′_Γ`'s is carried by `Z_Γ ∌ m` — contradiction (T1).
   So `ν = m`, or `a_Γ = 0` and `h_ν(γ) = 0` for all `γ ∈ Γ`.
4. **All of `U`.** If `ν ≠ m`, step 3 gives `h_ν(γ) = 0` for every `γ` in every admissible `Γ`; by Lemma W every non-torsion `u` lies in one,
   and torsion `u` has entropy 0. So:

> **PE-2, ergodic form (★)** [hand]. *Every `U`-invariant, `U`-ergodic probability measure `ν` on `R̂` is `m`, or has `h_ν(u) = 0` for every `u ∈ U`.*

5. **PE-2.** For a `U`-invariant finite positive `τ ≠ 0`, take its `U`-ergodic decomposition `τ = τ(R̂)∫ν dQ(ν)` (T1, `U` countable) and put
   `a = τ(R̂)Q({m})`, `τ′ = τ − a·m = τ(R̂)∫_{ν ≠ m} ν dQ`: finite, positive, `U`-invariant, and `h_{τ′}(u) = 0` for every `u` by (★) and T3.
   (`τ = 0`: `a = 0`, `τ′ = 0`.) ∎ **PE-2 is TRUE.**

## Stage 4 — SAVED (CE-1)

### 4.1 CE-1 — **TRUE only vacuously: its hypothesis is FALSE**, for every real number field `F`, on the very actions PE-1′ produces [hand; numeric sanity `tools/x02d_circle_measure.py`]

Call CE-1's hypothesis **(H)**: "every ergodic invariant probability measure of a totally irreducible ℤ²-action by automorphisms of a solenoid
with no virtually cyclic factor is Haar or finitely supported."

**(H) is false.** Take any real number field `F ⊆ ℝ`, so `L = F(i) ⊆ ℂ` and the conjugation `c` of `L/F` is complex conjugation; every `γ ∈ U`
has `|γ| = 1` in ℂ. Take `Γ` admissible (Lemma W) and `M = ℤ[Γ]`; by PE-1′ the `Γ`-action on `M̂` meets (H)'s hypotheses. For `λ ∈ ℂ` put
`χ_λ(x) = exp(2πi Re(λx))`, a character of `M`, and let `ν` be the image of uniform measure on `{|λ| = 1}` under `λ ↦ χ_λ`.
- *Invariant:* `γ·χ_λ = χ_{γλ}`, and `λ ↦ γλ` is a rotation of the circle.
- *Ergodic:* a non-torsion `γ` rotates by an irrational angle (`γ` is not a root of unity), so uniform measure is ergodic for it, hence for
  `Γ`; images of ergodic measures are ergodic.
- *Not Haar:* `ν̂(1) = ∫ exp(2πi Re λ) dλ = J₀(2π) = 0.2203… ≠ 0` (`2π` lies between `J₀`'s zeros 5.520 and 8.654), while Haar's coefficients
  vanish off 0.
- *Not finitely supported:* restricting to `ℤ·1 ⊂ M` maps `ν` to a measure on the circle group with coefficients `J₀(2πj) → 0`, so by Wiener's
  lemma it has no atoms, and neither has `ν`.

So (H) fails for every `F` (the numeric sanity and its two plants, `--plant-haar`, `--plant-atom`, both firing:
`data/fs100/x02d/circle_measure*.out`). **The mechanism:** every `u ∈ U` is an isometry at the complex place of `L` over each real embedding of
`F` — the "rotated plane" of rows 95–98 and G76 — so no element of `Γ` is hyperbolic, and the isometric direction carries invariant circles.
On `R̂` the same circle is a `U`-ergodic, non-Haar measure **carried by `A ⊆ E`**: exactly the measures MR excludes by `τ(E) = 0`.

**Which conjecture in print CE-1 would be conditional on.** EL2021 §1 states no conjecture (§1.2 above). The survey `[Lindenstrauss2004]`
(E. Lindenstrauss, *Rigidity of multiparameter actions*, arXiv:math/0402165, read pp. 1–6 at X02D; the copy is the one WebFetch saved,
md5 `7cddf1e1…`) states, verbatim: "Conjecture 1.3 (Furstenberg (1967)). Let n, m be two multiplicative independent positive integers. Any
Borel measure µ on T ergodic under the action of the semigroup generated by ×n , ×m is either Lebesgue measure or an atomic measure supported on
finitely many rational points." and, for higher-dimensional tori (§2, p. 6), in words only: "It is a conjecture of Katok and Spatzier that this
type of construction is the only source of non-algebraic α ergodic measures for a Zd action on a torus with d > 1." (the construction being a
lift from a lower-rank factor). Katok–Spatzier's own paper is titled, per EL2021's reference 22, "Invariant measures for higher-rank
hyperbolic abelian actions" (not read). And Berend's topological theorem, as EL2021 p. 2 quotes it, needs "contains a hyperbolic element".
**So (H) is the Katok–Spatzier conjecture moved from tori to solenoids with its hyperbolicity dropped, and that extension is false;** the
conjecture as it stands in print is about tori (and hyperbolic actions) and applies to none of our actions, whose elements are never
hyperbolic. **CE-1 as a route to MR is void.** A usable conditional must let the isometric (archimedean, and `p`-adically unit) directions
carry non-Haar zero-entropy measures — a measure analogue of Z. Wang's *Rigidity of commutative non-hyperbolic actions by toral
automorphisms* (EL2021 ref. 36; not held) — which is MR's own `E`-clause read at `Γ`. Proposed as a stub (§4.4).

### 4.2 What the derivation under (H) gives anyway, and a lemma that survives [hand, one mind]

Running §3.2's argument with (H) in place of E1 (a `Γ`-ergodic `ρ` on `R̂` has `ρ = m` or all projections to `M̂_k` finitely supported, since a
finitely supported measure never pushes to Haar), a `U`-ergodic `ν ≠ m` is carried by `LT_Γ = {χ : χ|_{ℤ[Γ]} has finite image}` for every
admissible `Γ` (a finite `Γ`-orbit on `M̂_k` consists of torsion characters: its points are fixed by some `γ ≠ 1`, so they kill `(γ − 1)M_k`, of
finite index by Lemma F). Then:

> **Lemma LT** [hand, one mind; given `R = O′` (row 100's Lemma C′)]. *`⋂_{Γ admissible} LT_Γ = T`.*

Proof sketch (adelic; `Σ` the split places, `𝔸_Σ` the restricted product over `Σ` and the archimedean places, `R̂ ≅ 𝔸_Σ/R*` with `R* ⊂ L` the dual
lattice — Tate/Weil duality, textbook). (i) `ℤ[Γ]` has finite index in `O_{Σ_Γ}` (`Σ_Γ` the finitely many places where `Γ` is not a unit): the
integral closure of the finitely generated ℤ-algebra `ℤ[Γ]` is `O_{Σ_Γ}` and is finite over it (Nagata), and Lemma F. (ii) So `ℤ[Γ]` is a
lattice in `V_Γ = ∏_{v ∈ Σ_Γ ∪ ∞} L_v`. (iii) For `χ = χ_y`, the part of `y` off `Σ_Γ ∪ ∞` has finite image on `ℤ[Γ]`, so `χ_y ∈ LT_Γ` iff
`y|_{V_Γ}` kills `N·K` for a finite-index `K ⊆ ℤ[Γ]` and some `N`, i.e. `y|_{V_Γ} = ℓ_Γ ∈ L` diagonally (annihilators of lattices of `L` lie in
`L`). (iv) `ℓ_Γ = y_∞` for every `Γ` (`L ↪ L ⊗ ℝ`), so `ℓ` is one element and `y − ℓ` vanishes at every place of every `Σ_Γ`; every split place
is in some `Σ_Γ` (the `u = z/c(z)`, `(z) = 𝔓ʰ`, of Lemma W, in an admissible `Γ`). So `y = ℓ ∈ L` and `χ` is torsion. ∎

Under (H) this would give "every `U`-invariant `τ` with `τ(T) = 0` is a multiple of `m`" — strictly stronger than MR (`T ⊊ E`), and refuted by
the circle measure of §4.1, carried by `A`: the chain from a false hypothesis ends at a false conclusion, a consistency check on both. **Lemma LT
itself does not use (H)** and is kept: a measure carried, for every admissible `Γ`, by characters torsion on `ℤ[Γ]` is carried by `T`. At the
fields of row 100's exception (a ramified stable dyadic place: ℚ(√5), ℚ(√17), ℚ(α)) step "R̂ ≅ 𝔸_Σ/R*" needs `O′/R` finite — a GAP there.
