Certified means, with every number an enclosure: 1. The constants. Each region's L is an arb upper bound of J26's formula, every factor enclosed: d_ij = √(a + b√3 + c√11 + d√33) from the exact squared distance; the weights from g23.csv as exact rationals; C from arb's J₁(10), Y₁(10); and sup_{u≥0}|J₁(u)| bounded by a ball-arithmetic grid on [0, 10] with a second-derivative remainder (|J₁″| ≤ 1, because J₁″ = (J₃ − 3J₁)/4 and |J_n| ≤ 1 by Bessel's integral), and by C/√u ≤ C/√10 on u ≥ 10. The inputs taken on citation are the two J26 took: Watson §13.74 (x(J₁² + Y₁²) decreasing), and d_ij ≥ 0.1 on the w2 pairs (re-checked here by a ball). 2. The points. Each point t of J26's grid, an exact dyadic, gets W(t) as an arb ball (arb's J₀, whose enclosures are rigorous). 3. The cells. For each consecutive a < b: LB(a, b) = (W̲(a) + W̲(b))/2 − L̄ (b − a)/2, computed exactly from the ball endpoints. Certified iff min LB ≥ 1 − µ = 0.99994 — every cell's lower enclosure plus the rigorous Lipschitz slack stays above the target. The three verdicts (exit codes fixed by the brief): - certified (exit 0): every cell's LB (from lower endpoints) is ≥ 0.99994. - violated (exit 2): some cell's margin is certified below zero — its LB computed from the *upper* endpoints is < 0.99994. That is a failure of J26's covering argument, not yet of H4; the tool reports separately whether some point value W(t) is certified below 0.99994 (which would falsify H4 itself at µ = 6·10⁻⁵).
