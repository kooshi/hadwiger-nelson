**SHARPENED 2026-09-09, by an instance inside a check written to enforce this very rule.** FS-20's "verified open" search walked the citations of `[Madore2015]` and **characterised each one by its abstract**. `[ExooIsmailescu2019]` was dismissed as *"a new proof of `χ(ℝ²) ≥ 5`"* — which its abstract says, and which its *conclusion* contradicts: three lines above its Madore citation it asks whether a 5-chromatic unit-distance graph fits in `ℚ(√3,√11)²`, the question Theorem B answers. **The paper was on our own shelf, held four days.** So the rule's scope widens: **an abstract is not a reading, and a citation list is not a literature review.** Two mechanical additions, both now in `WORKFLOW.md` step 3a and both cheap: **walk the citation graph of the paper that POSED the question, not only of the one that proved the nearest result** — Madore had four citations, the paper posing the question had forty-seven, and nobody had looked at them; and **cross-check citations in two databases**, because OpenAlex reported one citation of Madore where Semantic Scholar reported four. *What makes this the sharpest instance: the flawed check was itself the remedy for G49, written the day G50 was minted. A gate does not enforce itself, and the failure mode reappeared one level up — inside the check.*

**THE FALSIFIER DID NOT FIRE, AND THE §C DEBT COMPOUNDED TWICE MORE (2026-09-09, FS-23).** One day of reading three held papers and sweeping the "seen in a search listing only" section produced: **`[deGreyParts2022]`** (arXiv:2206.12635, sitting in §C as a bare title since 2026-09-06) — prior art for the whole M37–M43 cell family, from the dual direction, published in 2022; and **`[PartsOdd2022]`** (arXiv:2206.12632, in the same §C line) — which states the *observed* form of ledger row 59's 2-adic law, in print, naming `ℚ(√3,√11)` by name, in June 2022. Both were one `curl` away for three days. **That is three §C rows and two shelved PDFs that have turned out to be prior art for live work in eight days**, against a falsifier of "three milestones running whose §C scan turns up nothing". The rule stands and gains a corollary about SIZE: the useful part of each of these was one sentence of §1 or one row of a table, reachable in under five minutes, so **the cost of the scan is not the reason it keeps being skipped — the reason is that a title with a guess attached reads like knowledge.** A third mechanical addition, now `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md` § D rule 0f: **when a paper is read, its reference list is mined for titles in our neighbourhood and those go into §A as named-only rows the same day** — every one of `[Heule2021]`, `[Ardal2009]`, `[Exoo2005]`, `[Ivanov2007]`, `[Bukh2008]`, `[Maehara1991]` was found that way on 2026-09-09, and `[Heule2021]` is the closest published work to a theorem we had proved the day before.
