- **`./scripts/vacuity.sh` is green, and `./scripts/vacuity.sh --self-test` is green** — the check that a theorem is not a `∀` over an empty domain. Added 2026-09-09, after a theorem held since M34 turned out to say nothing and was noticed four weeks later only because somebody tried to *use* it. Deciding vacuity is undecidable, so the check does not try: it **requires evidence**, exactly as `axioms.sh` requires every trust purchase to be declared rather than verifying any proof.

**The law it implements is one law**: *a statement is vacuous exactly when some `∀`-binder of it has an empty domain*, and everything else follows from where the binder sits. A binder `(C : G.Coloring α)` is the original bug's shape. A binder `(h : Forces G k p q)` is **not**: an empty `Coloring` makes that hypothesis *true and free*, so the theorem asserts its conclusion outright — an **overclaim**, which is a different defect and is the deferred Phase C of `plans/STUB-vacuity-coverage.md`. A `Forces` **conclusion** needs no rule at all, because the reducing telescope unfolds it into the first case. *The first version special-cased `Forces`/`Separated`/`Fan` at every occurrence, and all 127 of them are binders — so that arm never once fired where it was right (FS-28).*

Six verdicts, and the vocabulary mirrors `axioms.sh` deliberately: `WITNESSED` is that gate's `CLEAN`, and `DECLARED` is its `PURCHASED` — **deliberate emptiness is not a defect but is never silent**, named per use with its reason in the declaration's own docstring (`VACUITY DECLARED:`) and printed under its own banner every run. Beside them: `VACUOUS` (provably empty and *undeclared* — the `core9_plane` bug), `STALE` (a docstring claims emptiness that is **not proved**, which is what makes the annotation a checked claim rather than a `# noqa`), `GENERIC` (a parametric carrier, where **no inhabitant witness can exist** and vacuity is the caller's obligation), and `UNKNOWN` (**reported and counted, never silent**). **`VACUOUS` and `STALE` are unconditional failures checked before the baseline; the baseline holds `UNKNOWN` and nothing else.** A **refutation is skipped structurally** — a declaration whose conclusion is `False` says *these cannot all hold*, so its binders are meant to be unsatisfiable, and flagging one is flagging a theorem for its own content.

**The counts, written here because a silent change in them is this gate's own failure mode** (FS-28 D1: a match arm shadowed by a catch-all dropped `witnessed` 8 → 1 and still printed a plausible report): `declarations=6222 refutations-skipped=231 sites=215`, `vacuous=0 stale=0 declared=3 witnessed=12 generic=198 unknown=2`. **The baseline holds two entries and nothing in it is dischargeable by anybody**: `hexShell_plane5` and `hexShell_plane6`, where proving the inhabitant *is* Hadwiger–Nelson. It went 15 → 4 → 2 in one day (FS-28, then FS-27 §1). It found `core9_plane` and `core122_plane` on its first run knowing nothing about them, and its residual independently re-flagged `core122_pigeonhole` — **two unrelated methods pointing at one declaration is the best evidence this check works**, and the follow-through closed the worry they shared.

**`--self-test` is the negative control, made mechanical**, because until 2026-09-09 this gate's controls could not be run at all: `isOurs` could not see declarations made in the file being elaborated, and `lake env lean` does not apply the lakefile's `autoImplicit := false`, so the first control that did run silently became a *different, generic* statement and returned a clean-looking wrong answer. Both repaired. Seven planted declarations, each watched to fire — and **the load-bearing one is the third**: the same carrier family at an instantiation nothing refutes, which must come back `UNKNOWN` and never `VACUOUS`, since a check that flags it is matching the family name rather than the instance and is worthless. The self-test's own failure path was watched too: breaking one control makes it name that control, print what it got instead, and exit 1;
