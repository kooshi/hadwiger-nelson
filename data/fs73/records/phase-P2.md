## Headline

- **K's picture recurs at three colours, and nowhere at four.**
  - Over ℚ(√11), the lattice spanned by the 36 unit vectors of denominator dividing 10 has **F = P = 3Λ**. This is verified
    independently (`4eba7998`, `data/fs73/records/verify-P2-q11.md`).
  - So every proper 3-colouring of its graph is 3Λ-periodic. It is then one of 48 affine colourings, which form one orbit under
    the point group. Given F = 3Λ, the three-colouring is **unique up to symmetry**.
  - The 100-vector lattice over ℚ(√11) (denominator dividing 50) also has F = P = 3Λ₅₀.
  - At four colours, de Grey's lattice (F = P = 8Λ, from P1) is the only rigid rank-4 CM lattice in the table. Every CM subfield
    of ℚ(√3,√5), ℚ(√3,√23) and ℚ(√3,√47), and every other CM subfield of K, has a **gapped periodic colouring**. So each is loose
    by theorem: F = P = 0 (FS70-T6).
- **ℚ(√167) is the table's informative instance, of a kind the plan did not predict.** Its three richest lattices (FS-61's
  shapes, with 60, 44 and 36 unit vectors) have **no periodic three-colouring at any admissible period N ≤ 12**, and L3e1c94c6 has
  none at any admissible N ≤ 20.
  - Every one of those refusals was re-asked by a second solver on a second encoding and agreed.
  - Yet FS-61 three-coloured a 1,474,541-point, degree-14 ball of L3e1c94c6 (`data/fs61/aiming-167.md`).
  - So either a larger ball refuses, which would give **χ(ℚ(√167)²) ≥ 4, FS-59's target**, or the lattice's colourings are
    periodic only at longer periods or not at all, which is **C3-aper's case** (R2).
- **The five-colour pilot is loose.** For k = 5, 25 of the 28 quartic CM subfields of K(√7)(i) are loose from their
  conjugation-fixed primes alone. The pilot, ℚ(√3,√−77), is the richest of the three survivors, and it is loose too: a gapped
  colouring at N = 7.

- **[hand] The fixed-prime gap lemma.**
  - *Statement:* let 𝔓 be a prime of a CM field E fixed by complex conjugation, and suppose its residue target (the Cayley
    graph of O_E/𝔓 on its norm-one circle) has a k-colouring with a fan gap. Then every lattice of E generated by unit vectors
    is loose at k colours: F = P = 0.
  - *Proof:*
    - Every unit vector is a 𝔓-unit, since v(z) + v(z̄) = 0 and v(z̄) = v(z). So the lattice is 𝔓-integral.
    - Reduction mod 𝔓 is then additive and sends U into the circle, hence closed neighbourhoods into closed neighbourhoods.
    - So the gap pulls back to a gapped periodic colouring, and FS70-T6 applies.
  - It is Theorem A's residue map (row 52) composed with FS70-T6.
  - *Where:* this record; `fs73_table.py` `fixed_targets`.

## Informative instances (for the competition's §C)

1. **ℚ(√167), L3e1c94c6** (36 unit vectors, M = 120, rank 4, wall-broken, 3-non-integral).
   - It has no periodic 3-colouring at admissible N ∈ {4, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20}. The other N ≤ 20 are
     inadmissible.
   - Its 1.47-million-point ball three-colours (FS-61).
   - **Either it is not 3-colourable**, so some finite ball refuses and χ(ℚ(√167)²) ≥ 4: FS-59's first landing. **Or it is a
     3-colourable lattice graph with no short-period colouring**: a C3-aper candidate (`[AbrishamiEtAl2025]` 4.6 at ℤ⁴).
   - L21cc6506 (60 unit vectors) and Ld14c588b (44) behave the same at N ≤ 12.
2. **ℚ(√11), the second positive instance of C3-inherit (F = P = 3Λ), at three colours.**
   - It is informative *as a positive*, not as a gap. Unlike K's 8/3, its H1 is not known only because forcing holds: FS-58's
     screens established the affine-only periodic colouring space independently of any forcer.
3. **No lattice in the table has P_found of full rank with F_known of lower rank and forcing undecided.** The two full-rank
   P_found lattices at k = 4 and k = 3 both close to F = P.

**A reading, offered for §C and G54, not claimed.**
- *The reading:* full-rank F appears exactly where the lattice is integral at a conjugation-fixed prime whose residue target is
  Hoffman-tight at k, and no other prime's quotient has a gap. F is then a level of that prime's tower.
- *The instances:*
  - K is 2-integral with U₂ × U₂ = K₄ × K₄ (G70), tight at 4, and F = 8Λ.
  - ℚ(√11)'s Λ₁₀ is 3-integral into the 3-adic plane, tight at 3 (FS58-T1), and F = 3Λ.
- *Killing test:* a lattice integral at a tight fixed prime, with no gapped quotient at N ≤ 12, whose F is not full rank. Or a
  full-rank F with no tight integral prime.

## Defects found in this phase's own work (G21: each flattering or silent until caught)

1. The spectral label read **"tight-or-below"** for the slack case, inverting G54's sign. Fixed. The report derives the label
   from the stored numbers, and `spectral_sign` documents which rows carry the old label.
2. **BFGS alone** read "Hoffman-refuses" on a lattice with a verified period-3 three-colouring, which is impossible. BFGS had
   found a local minimum. The column now takes the least of BFGS and the exact quotient minima.
3. `fs73_screen.PColour.kper` skips colour pairs with one unused colour. That is an over-approximation of KPer (still a sound
   upper bound). `kper_k` returns 0 there instead, and the plant `kper_skip_unused` shows the difference.
4. My first pgid file recorded the launching shell, not the job's group. It was caught before any kill, and the kill used the
   right group, verified by effect.
5. **My F_known = 0 on ℚ(√11) measured the forcers I had imported, not the lattice.** The PO caught it: FS-58's forcers lived on
   exactly this lattice.
