# FS-73 — the owner's blind controls (written 2026-09-23 before any brief; NEVER briefed; scored against §G/§PA at the plan review)

B1. Compactness splits C3. Cay(Λ, U) is locally finite, so "some finite ball forces (0, δ)" ⟺ "every proper 4-colouring of the
    whole lattice identifies 0 and δ" (König / Tychonoff). C3's content is therefore PERIODIC colourings vs ALL colourings.
B2. Recognition in another discipline: proper 4-colourings of Cay(ℤ^r, U) form a ℤ^r subshift of finite type (a hom-shift to K4).
    C3 holds whenever periodic points are dense in that shift (the cylinder {c(0) ≠ c(δ)} is open). Symbolic dynamics:
    Berger 1966 / Robinson 1971 (aperiodic SFTs exist for r ≥ 2, so no general positive answer); hom-shifts (Chandgotia–Marcus).
    G30's discipline ladder has no symbolic-dynamics room — expect §PA to enter it.
B3. Rank 1 is trivial (a symmetric ℤ-SFT; periodic points dense); the question is genuinely higher-rank.
B4. G66's chain: periodic colourings = continuous (clopen) colourings of the profinite completion Λ̂; C3 is the compulsion analogue
    of χ_clopen vs χ — "forced under clopen colourings ⟹ forced under all". Expect G66 marked BEARS.
B5. G70 over K: a √3-clean colouring of the Moser lattice IS a residue (periodic) colouring, so every non-periodic colouring carries a
    monochromatic √3-pair — a structural route to C3 over K, reducing it to the √3-dirty colourings. Expect G70 BEARS with this.
B6. G21: "rigid under every periodic colouring FOUND" measures the finder; the plan must separate the theorem's hypothesis (every
    periodic colouring, at every period) from what a screen can certify (every colouring at the periods asked).
B7. Controls: Harder's 23,083-point object over K is the positive control; FS-72's rank-8 lattice over ℚ(√3,√5) (no periodic
    colouring found) is where a counterexample could live; G68's gapped quotient colourings are the negative control (free by row 66).
