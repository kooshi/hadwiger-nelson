# data/m52 — durable outputs of M52 (the copy-lock objects)

Created 2026-09-02 at the M52 subplanning (`plans/M52-copy-lock-objects.md`). The descent chain and
the Q8 recipes stay in `data/m51/`; this directory holds what M52 produces that could not be
re-derived cheaply: the atlas tables (`atlas.json`), each composition's recipe and its culled kept
set, the shrunk object's recipe, and the scored predictions. Refreshed at every phase close.
