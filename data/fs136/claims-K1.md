## Definitions

**Definitions, stated once.** `c` is a weak six-colouring of an open set `U`. A **piece** is a connected open subset of `U` almost every point of which has one label. A **pieced point** is a point `x` of `U` with a neighbourhood that is, up to a null set, the union of finitely many disjoint pieces, each with `x` in its closure; its **labels** are its pieces' labels (a three-piece point of FS-124 is one with three pieces and three labels). For `|w − x| = 1` a piece of `x` is **inner at `w`** if it lies in the closed unit disc about `w`, **outer at `w`** if it misses the open one, **crossing** otherwise; `x` **separates at `w`** if it has an inner and an outer piece there (`plans/FS124-E2-derivation.md` § Notation). A **covering pair** is two pieced points `x`, `y` with `0 < |x − y| < 2` such that each of the six labels is a label of `x` or of `y`. Its **common unit points** are the two points one unit from both. For a point `p`, a covering pair **on the ring of `p`** is one with `|x − p| = |y − p| = 1`; `p` is then one common unit point and **the point across** is the other.

**Added by this plan.** A **family of pieces at `x`** is a finite set of pieces of a weak `k`-colouring, each with `x` in its closure (not assumed disjoint, of different labels, or filling a neighbourhood; the pieces of a pieced point are one). For `|w − x| = 1` a label `a` of the family is **inner-pure at `w`** if every piece of the family with label `a` is inner at `w`, **outer-pure at `w`** if every one is outer, **barred at `w`** otherwise. No label is both (a non-empty open set lies on no circle). `x` **separates at `w` by labels** if the family has an inner-pure label and an outer-pure label.

**Added by this round.** Let `x` be a point and `γ₁ ≠ γ₂` two curves from `x`, each a ray from `x` or an arc of a circle starting at `x`. For `ρ > 0`, `Σ_ρ(x; γ₁, γ₂)` is the set of points of `B(x, ρ) ∖ {x}` strictly between `γ₁` and `γ₂`, counter-clockwise from `γ₁`. A **corner at `x`** is such a pair of curves; a **labelled corner** is a corner with a label. A weak `k`-colouring `c` of `U` **has** the labelled corner `(x; γ₁, γ₂; a)` if for some `ρ > 0` the set `Σ_ρ` lies in `U` and almost every point of it has the label `a`. For `|w − x| = 1` a corner at `x` is **inner at `w`** if `Σ_ρ` lies in the closed unit disc about `w` for some `ρ > 0`, **outer at `w`** if `Σ_ρ` misses the open unit disc about `w` for some `ρ > 0`, and **crossing at `w`** otherwise. For distinct points `x_1, …, x_m` (`m ≥ 1`) one unit from `w`, **the corners at `w` cut by `S(x_1), …, S(x_m)`** are the `2m` corners at `w` between cyclically consecutive branches at `w` of those circles. A finite set `F` of labelled corners **forces** `(w, Σ, ℓ)` if `Σ` is a corner at `w` cut by the unit circles about the points of `F` one unit from `w` (there is at least one), and every label other than `ℓ` is the label of a corner of `F` at such a point `x` that is not inner at `w` when `Σ` is inside `S(x)`, and not outer at `w` when `Σ` is outside `S(x)`; `F` **empties** `Σ` if every label is. The **forced closure in `U`** of a set `𝒮` of labelled corners is the least set of labelled corners that contains `𝒮` and every `(w, Σ, ℓ)` with `w ∈ U` forced by a finite subset of it.

## The statements, in the order to take them

**C136-TABLE** — statement: Let c be a weak k-colouring of an open set U, w a point of U, x_1, …, x_m distinct points with |x_i − w| = 1, and 𝒫_i a family of pieces at x_i. Then there is η > 0 such that almost every point z of B(w, η) has a label a with the property: for every i such that a is a label of 𝒫_i, a is inner-pure at w for 𝒫_i if |z − x_i| < 1, and outer-pure at w for 𝒫_i if |z − x_i| > 1.

*Not claimed (C136-TABLE):* that a weak colouring has a piece; anything about a label of no 𝒫_i (it is unconstrained); a converse — a label left at every cell does not give a colouring; the largest η (it is half the least witness depth: for a piece crossing by a thin horn it is of the order of the horn's width)

**C136-COVER** — statement: Let c be a weak six-colouring of U, x, y points with 0 < |x − y| < 2, 𝒫_x, 𝒫_y families of pieces at x and at y such that each of the six labels is a label of 𝒫_x or of 𝒫_y, and w a point of U with |w − x| = |w − y| = 1. Then x or y separates at w by labels: one of the two families has a label all of whose pieces are inner at w and another label all of whose pieces are outer at w.

**C136-ONE** — statement: Let c be a weak six-colouring of U, p a point with a family 𝒫 of pieces at p whose labels are all six, and w a point of U with |w − p| = 1.
