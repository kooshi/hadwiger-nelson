   Is every statement of yours that concludes "a colour in two sectors" true for it? (This point is A2's own sketch, one mind; it is not a side's

   you hold a classification of how a vertex blocks near w₀, check that the generic three-sector vertex (a one-sided block of depth ≍ s) is an entry,
