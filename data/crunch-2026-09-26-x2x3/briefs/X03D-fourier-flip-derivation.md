## The question, and why now

MR(F) is "every `U`-invariant finite positive measure on `R̂` with `τ(E) = 0` is a multiple of Haar measure" (J38 §3.5, T-J38-2 (iii)).
Three questions sit under it. None needs entropy.

1. **The Fourier form (FR-1).** If `x x̄ = y ȳ ≠ 0` then `y/x` has norm one, so `y/x ∈ U`. A `U`-invariant `τ` then has `τ̂` constant on
   each fibre of the quadrance. MR becomes a Schoenberg-type statement: every positive-definite function of the quadrance on `R`, with an
   `E`-null spectral measure, is `c·δ₀`. The classical analogue on 𝕋 is measures invariant under every `×n`. It is trivial, because
   `ℕ` has one orbit on the positive integers.
2. **An averaging route (ED-1, ED-2).** For `U`-invariant `τ`, `τ̂(1)` is the average of `τ̂(u)` over any finite `U_N`, that is
   `∫ (1/#U_N) Σ_{u∈U_N} χ(u) dτ(χ)`. If the Weyl averages of every `χ ∉ E` over the circle's rational points tend to 0, MR follows with no
   rigidity theorem at all. **The planner's named risk:** a character that is non-integral only at a sparse set of split primes `P`, with
   `Σ_{p∈P} 1/p < ∞`. There the product of local factors may not vanish, and ED-2 may be FALSE. If it is FALSE, name the refined
   exceptional set, and ask whether a `U`-invariant finite measure can charge it outside `E`. (A recurrence argument at the split places,
   where `U` acts hyperbolically, may exclude that; see the advisor's sketch in X01D's brief, which you may read.)
3. **The flip (G79; FL-1, FL-2).** What does the spectral measure of every *actual* four-colouring carry that an arbitrary invariant
   measure need not? Here is the planner's candidate. Properness gives `σ̂(u) = −1/3` on all of `U` (the tetrahedral embedding). Rows
   112–113 bound what an `E`-carried part can contribute at unit distance, since every factor there has ratio `< 1/4`. Together these force
   the non-`E` part `τ` to be at least as negatively correlated on the unit circle as a four-colouring. If FL-1 holds, the consumer needs
   only FL-2's weaker hypothesis, a Hoffman-type bound on `E`-null spectra, and not MR itself. That is a smaller target, and maybe a
   different discipline's.

Every outcome counts: a FALSE with its counterexample, or a GAP with the step named, is as useful as a TRUE.
