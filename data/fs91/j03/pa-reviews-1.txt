== 1243.05082 2011 Euclidean distance graphs on the rational points
[review] Let \(X\) be a subset of \(\mathbb R^n\) and let \(D\) be a set of positive real numbers. The Euclidean distance graph \(G(X,D)\) is the graph with vertex set \(X\), and with \(x,y\in X\) adjacent only when \(|x-y|\in D\), where \(|x-y|\) is the Euclidean distance of \(x\) and \(y\). Let \(\chi(X,D)\) be the chromatic number of \(G(X,D)\). The paper is mainly a survey of problems and results on \(\chi(\mathbb Q^n,\{d\})\).

For the entire collection see [Zbl 1202.05004].

== 0974.05029 2000 Problems posed in or arising from ``Colorings of metric spaces'': Status report
[review] The present article is a survey of results contained in the paper ``Colorings of metric spaces'' by \textit{M. Benda} and \textit{M. Perles} (written in 1976 and circulated in preprint form, but published only recently [Geombinatorics 9, No. 3, 113-126 (2000; Zbl 0951.05037)]), as well as some subsequent developments on open problems posed in it. Given a metric space \(X\) and a positive number \(r\), the graph \((X,r)\) has vertices the points of \(X\), with \(x\) and \(y\) joined by an edge iff the distance between \(x\) and \(y\) is \(r\). The problems discussed in the article are concerned primarily with determining the chromatic number and the connectivity of graphs of the form \((X,r)\). Specifically, (1) determine the chromatic number of \((Q^n,1)\) for \(n \geq 5\), and of \((X,1)\), when \(X\) is a finite algebraic extension of \(Q^2\), (2) characterize the subgroup of \(Q^n\) generated by the elements of length 1 (\((Q^n,1)\) is connected if and only if this group is all of \(Q^n\)), and similarly for \(F^n\), when \(F\) is a finite real algebraic extension of \(Q\) and (3) determine the connectivity of \((F^n,1)\), when \(F\) is either \(Q(\sqrt{n_1},\dots,\sqrt{n_t})\) with \(n_1,\dots,n_t \in Q\) or \(Q(\alpha)\), where \(\alpha\) is a real algebraic number.

== 1140.05303 2007 Circular coloring the plane
[summary] Summary: The unit distance graph \(\mathcal{R}\) is the graph with vertex set \(\mathbb{R}^2\) in which two vertices (points in the plane) are adjacent if and only if they are at Euclidean distance 1. We prove that the circular chromatic number of \(\mathcal{R}\) is at least 4, thus improving the known lower bound of \(32/9\) obtained from the fractional chromatic number of \(\mathcal{R}\).

== 1213.05061 2010 Distance graphs from \(p\)-adic norms
[review] Motivated by the famous Hadwiger-Nelson problem that asks for the minimum number of colors needed to color all points of the real plane so that two points at distance 1 do not have the same color, many authors have studied the chromatic number of the distance graph with the set of integers as its vertex set and \(xy\) being an edge if \(|x-y| \in D\), where \(D\) is a set of positive integers. In this paper, using \(p\)-adic methods, upper bounds on the chromatic number of the distance graph in terms of divisibility properties of elements in \(D\) are provided.
