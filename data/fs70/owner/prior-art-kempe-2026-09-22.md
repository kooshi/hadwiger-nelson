## 1. The instrument, in the discipline's words

*Kempe chains* (Kempe 1879) of a proper colouring of an infinite vertex-transitive graph — here the unit-distance graph of a
lattice `Λ ⊂ ℂ` — started from a proper **additive** colouring (a group homomorphism `φ : Λ → ℤ/4` or the Klein group, nonzero
on the unit vectors; `[Fischer1990]`'s framework, `[Polymath16wiki]`'s "linear colouring"). The claim: the `(0, b)`-Kempe
component of the origin has colour-`0` part equal to the subgroup generated by the pair sums `z + w` over unit vectors with
`φ(z) = b`, `φ(w) = −b`; a Kempe swap on it is a proper, non-additive colouring; if the subgroup misses `δ` (with `φ(δ) = 0`) the
swap separates `(0, δ)`, on every finite subgraph, so no finite graph in `Λ` forces `(0, δ)` at any radius. Its vocabulary
elsewhere: *Kempe equivalence* of colourings (Las Vergnas–Meyniel 1981; Mohar 2006; Bonamy–Bousquet–Feghali–Johnson 2019 on
regular graphs), *recolouring / reconfiguration* of colourings, *frozen colourings*, *mixing* of colourings on lattices.

## 3. The prior art that IS there — in our own corpus, unread until today

**`[Polymath16wikiR2]` — Polymath16's "Coloring R_2" page (June 2018)**, held in the bibliography since FS-23 as *"not read — a
blocking acquisition for FS-20"*, a copy of which had been fetched into this session's scratchpad and never opened. Read in full
today (archived: `~/source/library/notes/polymath16-coloring-R2-2018.txt`; bibliography row rewritten). It is **the hand form of
the question the Kempe screen answers computationally**: for a 4-colouring `c` valued in `R₁/2R₁ ≅ 𝔽₄` (`R₁` the Eisenstein
integers), the *renormalised colouring* `ρ_z(a) = c(z + a) + a` measures the deviation from the residue colouring on each
translate `z + R₁`; the page proves the colour classes of `ρ_z` are convex polygons in the triangular lattice (Lemma 1),
classifies the three-valued case as periodic or **tri-sector** (Prop. 6 — the non-additive proper colourings of an Eisenstein
translate, the very objects a Kempe swap produces), and proves **Corollary 9: a colouring `2R₁`-periodic on `R₁` is `2R₁`-periodic
on `R₁ + ρR₁`** (`ρ = η²` the Moser twist) — additive rigidity propagating from the Eisenstein lattice to the first ring of de
Grey's lattice over `K`. It stops there.

**How it bears on FS-70's finding.** Over `K` our Kempe screen reads de Grey's rank-4 lattice **rigid** (24 additive
colourings, none with an escape at `8/3`, `po_kempe_control_K.py`) — the computational statement of the rigidity whose first
step Corollary 9 proves by hand; Harder's `8/3` forcer lives on that rigidity. Over `ℚ(√3,√5)` the analogue **fails**: the faithful
rank-4 transposition `⟨ζ₆, u⟩` has 72 additive colourings with Kempe escapes at `4/3`, `8/3` and `4`, because `u` has no square
root in the field where `ρ` has `η` (review-phase1 §2.3). So the milestone's mechanism finding can be stated in Polymath's terms:
**the "Coloring R_2" propagation (Cor. 9) has no analogue over ℚ(√3,√5), and the Kempe screen is the instrument that measures
where it fails.** For the close: the Kempe screen is ours as an instrument (finite, exact, with a falsification control); the
rigidity question it answers was posed and half-answered by hand over `K` in 2018; the tri-sector classification is theirs.
