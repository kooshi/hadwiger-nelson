## The question

M1 (FS-91) proved, by hand and with blind checks, results about **character colourings** — colourings of a field's unit-distance plane
of the form `x ↦ ⌊kθ(x)⌋` for a homomorphism `θ` to `ℝ/ℤ`. Before anything is called new, the world's literature must be asked, in the
discipline's words. The headline results and their records (read the statements there; the index `plans/CRUNCH-2026-09-25-INDEX.md` §2
lists them all):
1. **Band rigidity at every band** (J01, J03a): for `|S| ≥ 2`, every closed `U_S`-invariant set of characters of `ℤ[i][1/p : p ∈ S]`
   missing a neighbourhood of 0 lies in torsion plus archimedean — `plans/FS91-S0V2-SWm-second-reading.md`. Disciplines: topological
   dynamics (Furstenberg ×2×3, Berend's ID property, S-arithmetic / multiplicative semigroup actions on solenoids), harmonic analysis.
2. **The class theorem CLG** (J07, J10): a character colouring of a real number field's plane exists with `k` colours iff one exists on
   the residue plane at a single stable place — `plans/FS91-S1D-character.md`. Disciplines: circular colourings, Cayley graphs of
   abelian groups, "chromatic number of abelian Cayley graphs", local–global principles, Hasse principle.
3. **Three colours** (J02, J09): for every real number field, the plane is three-colourable iff the local bound is at most three —
   `plans/FS91-S0F-three-colours.md`. `[Fischer1990]` §1 poses the general question verbatim (the records say); `[Johnson2000]`,
   `[Johnson2006]` are held-unread debts.
4. **Lemma C/C′** (J07, J10): the ring generated by the unit vectors, `R_F/NR_F` — orders generated by a norm-one torus's rational points
   (algebraic number theory: norm-one tori, class field theory, "ring generated by units of norm one").
5. **The peeling lemma** and **J21-MX** (M3's, if it bears — a spectral form of Burr–Erdős–Lovász `χ(G × K_n) = min`; Hedetniemi for
   special factors).
6. **One prime** (J12): at a single prime, a minimal set of characters outside torsion plus archimedean exists, and torsion orders are
   unbounded — `plans/FS91-S1D-one-prime.md`. Disciplines named in J12's record: piecewise / discretized rotations with rational cosine
   (Adler–Kitchens–Tresser; Goetz; Buzzi; Bosio–Vivaldi 2000 "Round-off errors and p-adic numbers"; Akiyama–Pethő 2013), S-adic
   minimality.
7. **Six at the exposed fields** (J08, J11): the fewest colours of a character colouring of `UD(𝔽_q, 2)` — "circular chromatic number of
   finite Euclidean planes", Kloosterman sums, trace sets.
