## Plain summary

The plane over the number field ℚ(√167) — the field where the project's local–global conjecture has its widest gap (predicted to need at
least six colours; four was proven by hand and by a checker) — now has "needs at least four colours" checked by Lean's kernel, on the
standard three axioms and with no trusted native code. No finite unit-distance graph is built: a proper three-colouring of that plane would
hand an integer "winding" to 18 unit vectors, and a 1,050-leaf certificate (already on disk, transformed exactly to integer multipliers)
shows no winding exists. The kernel checks the certificate in about 12 CPU seconds.

The three pieces are reusable: the winding theorem (any abelian group, any generators), the certificate checker with its soundness proof,
and a one-line consumer that turns any passing certificate in this format into "χ(F²) ≥ 4" for every real field F containing √d.

Every registered prediction that was run came out as predicted. The whole job took about 45 minutes of wall clock against a plan of about
4.5 hours; the step the plan named as its one real risk (the proof over the nested certificate tree) went through by mutual structural
recursion on the second elaboration. Two audits went further than the brief asked: the two hypotheses it named are shown necessary (a
counterexample for each), not just used. ℚ(√47)'s certificate turned out to be in a different format (a tree over the character torus, for
another checker), so it was not attempted.
