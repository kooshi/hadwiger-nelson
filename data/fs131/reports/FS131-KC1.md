## What was done, in plain words

The brief asked for typed statements, a skeleton and a plan per leaf. The leaves closed while typing, so the hand-off is
a finished module: `Proofs/HadwigerNelson/Plane/LebesgueNumber.lean`, about 430 lines, no `sorry`.

- **The lemma.** `[Davies2026]` Lemma B.1 (p. 44) is formalized with its printed six-step proof. Its core is the
  sentence the paper introduces it with: a Lebesgue-measurable colouring that is proper on a conull set can be changed on
  a null set to one proper everywhere. Proof in Lean as on the page: almost every point is a density-one point of its own
  colour class (Mathlib's Besicovitch density theorem); finitely many sets of density one at a point meet in a set of
  density one, so any finite set of points can be slid by one translation into the good set with its good points keeping
  their colours; Rado's selection principle (Mathlib's form of the compactness principle) glues these finite colourings
  into one, equal to the given colouring on the good set.
- **The density-point step** was already in the library (`Plane.AeColouring.dist_ne_one`); here it is four short lemmas
  about `AeColouring.core`.
- **The number.** `Plane.chiLebesgue` is the least `k` with a Lebesgue-measurable `k`-colouring of the plane proper at
  every unit pair. `chiM0 = chiLebesgue` needs no outside theorem; `χ(ℝ²) = chiLebesgue` rests on the one declared outside
  theorem. Beside them: `χ(ℝ²) ≤ chiLebesgue ≤ chiM` with no import, where `chiM` is the Borel number.
