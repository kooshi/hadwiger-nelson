Results in detail

This file is the reference behind README.md. For every result the front page names, it gives the statement as a plain-words reading of the stored Lean type, the Lean names, the axioms each rests on, whose mathematics it is, and the row of LEDGER.md that records its grade and sources. A list of references closes the file.

How the axiom lists were made. Each list is exactly what #print axioms printed. They were printed from the library as gated on 2026-10-15 at commit 752b11569 (the gate ran before the publication's rewrite, which changed one docstring in the proofs and nothing else in them), and for the results changed since, at the commit each landing gated: 113ca8ece (the main clause through five as one theorem), 7c72b9bf7 (a number field's plane equal to the plane's), and e3e1dbb73 (the p-adic plane, merged as 785a46e77). All of them were printed again on 2026-10-16 from main's build, with the same result; the scratch files and their output are in data/fs131/rdm1/.

Conventions. Every Lean name is under the namespace HadwigerNelson. std3 means [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], Lean's three standard axioms. W, EL and O5 are the three published theorems the library declares as axioms (README § What the library assumes). Keys in brackets, like [Fischer1990], are entries of BIBLIOGRAPHY.md; the references below give each in full. "Prior art: not found in print" reports the searches recorded in the ledger; it is not a claim of priority.

Definitions the statements use

  • The plane over a field. For a subfield F ⊆ ℝ, F² is the graph on {z ∈ ℂ : Re z, Im z ∈ F} (Lean: planeOver F) joining points at Euclidean distance exactly 1 (unitDistance ℂ), and χ(F²) its chromatic number. The plane itself is unitDistance ℂ, or unitDistance (EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 2)) in the oldest theorems.
  • ℚ(√m) is SquareClass.fieldOfRadicands {m}, the subfield of ℝ generated by √m; ℚ(√3, √5) is fieldOfRadicands {3, 5}, and so on.
  • L(F) (SpectralChain.localBound), for a real number field F: the infimum, over the maximal ideals P of the ring of integers 𝓞 of F(i) with conj⁻¹(P) = P (SpectralChain.StablePrime F), of the chromatic number of the graph on 𝓞/P joining x and y when (x − y)·conj(x − y) = 1 (levelTarget P 1). It is ⊤ if there is no such prime.
  • Uniform thickness (Collapse.Thick F c): for every such P, every m ≥ 1 and every non-trivial additive character ψ of 𝓞/Pᵐ, ‖Σ ψ(s)‖ ≤ c · N, the sum running over the N elements s of 𝓞/Pᵐ with s·conj(s) = 1 (Rigidity.Counting.normOneQ P m). Collapse.ThickAt P c is the same at one prime.
  • The unit-vector ring of F (SpectralChain.unitRing F): the subring of ℂ generated by the points of F² at distance 1 from the origin.
  • χ_m⁰(ℝ²) (Plane.chiM0): the least k with a Borel map ℂ → Fin k each of whose colour classes has a null set of unit pairs (Plane.AeColouring k). χ_m(ℝ²) (Plane.chiLebesgue): Falconer's measurable chromatic number, the least k with a Lebesgue-measurable colouring proper at every unit pair.
  • A weak colouring of an open set U (Plane.IsWeakColouringOn U c): a Borel map c : ℂ → Fin k whose monochromatic unit pairs inside U, as pairs (x, θ) with x and x + e^{iθ} in U, form a null set for area times arc length.
  • µ_k(ε) (Plane.mu k ε): the supremum, over k measurable pairwise disjoint sets each of whose pairs at distances in [1 − ε, 1 + ε] form a null set of ℂ × ℂ, of the upper density of their union about the origin.
  • m₁(ℝ²) (Plane.mOne): the supremum of the upper densities about the origin of the measurable sets with no two points at distance 1. α₁(G) (SimpleGraph.finIndepRatio): the infimum over non-empty finite vertex sets S of α(G[S])/|S|. f(n) (Plane.minIndepNum n): the least independence number of the unit-distance graph on n points of the plane. The algebraic plane 𝒜²: unitDistance on Outside.OAI158.Spectral.E, the algebraic numbers in ℂ.
  • The p-adic plane (PadicPlane.padicPlane p): the graph on ℚ_p × ℚ_p joining x and y when (x₁ − y₁)² + (x₂ − y₂)² = 1. χ_Bor (PadicPlane.borelChromaticNumber): the least k with a colouring all of whose k colour classes are measurable sets.

This project's results

1. χ(ℚ(√47)²) = 5, and four more fields

  • Statement. χ(ℚ(√47)²) = 5. Also χ = 5 over ℚ(√143), ℚ(√311), ℚ(√335) and ℚ(√3, √5), and χ(ℚ(√167)²) ≥ 5.
  • Lean. SqrtFortySeven.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt47_eq_five; SqrtClassValues.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt143_eq_five, SqrtClassValues.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt311_eq_five, SqrtClassValues.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt335_eq_five, SqrtClassValues.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt3_sqrt5_eq_five, SqrtClassValues.five_le_chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt167. The upper half alone: SqrtFortySeven.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt47_le_five.
  • Axioms. std3 + W + EL. The upper half: std3.
  • Whose. The lower half: prior art not found in print. The upper half ≤ 5 is prior art (the plane at 11). [Moorhouse2010slides] asks for χ(ℚ(√47)²) by name. [Davies2026] p. 5 asks the question by residue class modulo 168, and the field of √47 lies in one of those classes. It answers the existence half of [Soifer2024] Open Problem 55.1, which asks for a construction, and [ExooIsmailescu2019]'s smallest-subfield question in part. No finite five-chromatic graph is exhibited.
  • Ledger. Rows 121, 123. The family (every squarefree d ≡ 47, 143, 167 (mod 168) with L = 5, 44 of the 52 below 3000), row 123's class statement in full, and the field ℚ(√23, ∛2) are proved by hand and not stored as Lean theorems (ROADMAP.md § 3).

2. χ(F²) = L(F) whenever L(F) ≤ 5

  • Statement. For every real number field F: min(L(F), 5) ≤ χ(F²), with no further hypothesis. With the upper bound χ(F²) ≤ L(F) it gives χ(F²) = L(F) whenever L(F) ≤ 5, and, with no hypothesis, min(χ(F²), 5) = min(L(F), 5).
  • Lean. Rigidity.min_localBound_five_le_chromaticNumber, Rigidity.chromaticNumber_planeOver_eq_localBound_of_le_five, Rigidity.min_chromaticNumber_five_eq_min_localBound_five.
  • Axioms. std3 + EL + W.
  • Whose. The local–global conjecture χ(F²) = L(F) is this project's. The question is [Fischer1990] §1's and [Johnson2000]'s Problem 4.4. Measure rigidity for the unit-vector group of a number field, and the five: prior art not found in print. The route from EL to the number field (two deductions of this project's) is kernel-checked here.
  • Ledger. Row 123 (and row 99 below five).

3. Three colours

  • Statement. For a real number field F whose plane is not 2-colourable, χ(F²) = 3 exactly when some maximal ideal of the ring of integers of F has residue field 𝔽₃ (three elements). χ(F²) ≤ 3 ⟺ L(F) ≤ 3. And χ(F²) = L(F) whenever L(F) ≤ 4.
  • Lean. ThreeFourColours.chromaticNumber_planeOver_eq_three_iff, …_le_three_iff, …_eq_localBound_of_le_four.
  • Axioms. std3 + W.
  • Whose. The question is [Fischer1990] §1's: is there an algebraic criterion for χ(F²)? It is also [Johnson2000]'s Problem 4.4. The answer at this range: prior art not found in print. The two-colour classification over every real number field and the three-colour one over fields containing √3 are GPT Astra's (an OpenAI model, 2026-09-14). Weil's bound is read at one place, in the case of three colours; the direction from the prime to the colouring rests on std3.
  • Ledger. Row 99. "No mixing" and χ(ℚ(α, √7)²) = 4, α³ = α + 1, stay hand.

4. Four colours over real quadratic fields

  • Statement. For every natural number d ≡ 11 (mod 12), χ(ℚ(√d)²) ≥ 4; the same for every subfield of ℝ holding √d. For every subfield F ⊆ ℝ whose unit-vector ring holds 1/3: if F² is not 2-colourable then χ(F²) ≥ 4, and in any case χ(F²) ≠ 3. For squarefree m, χ(ℚ(√m)²) is 2 when m ≢ 3 (mod 4) and 3 when m ≡ 3, 7 (mod 12); when m ≡ 11 (mod 12) it is at least 4, and exactly 4 unless m ≡ 47, 143, 167 (mod 168). And χ(ℚ(√11)²) = 4, its lower half also by an explicit 204-point triangle-free graph.
  • Lean. TorsionExtraction.four_le_chromaticNumber_planeOver_fieldOfRadicands, TorsionExtraction.four_le_chromaticNumber_planeOver_of_sqrt_mem, TorsionExtraction.four_le_chromaticNumber_planeOver_of_inv_three_mem, TorsionExtraction.chromaticNumber_planeOver_ne_three_of_inv_three_mem, ThreeFourColours.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt_table; UpperHalf.chromaticNumber_planeOver_eleven, four_le_chromaticNumber_planeOver_eleven (the explicit graph).
  • Axioms. std3.
  • Whose. The values 2 and 3 and the upper bounds are [Fischer1990]'s (Thms 8, 9, 10(i)); the identity behind 1/3 at m ≡ 23 (mod 24) is his Thm 10(ii), which concludes only that no additive colouring with few colours exists. The lower bound of four, and the step from additive colourings to all colourings: prior art not found in print. [Johnson2006] p. 214 asks whether any m needs four; [Johnson2000] p. 176 calls the family open. For ℚ(√11) the upper bound is [Moorhouse2010]'s and [Fischer1990]'s.
  • Ledger. Rows 73, 82, 99.

5. A number field's plane needs as many colours as the plane

  • Statement. χ(F²) = χ(ℝ²) for every real number field whose conjugation-fixed primes are uniformly c-thick with 0 ≤ c < 1/5. So χ(ℚ(√635087)²) = χ(ℝ²) and χ(ℚ(√3, √5, √7, √11)²) = χ(ℝ²), and with O5, 6 ≤ χ(ℚ(√635087)²) ≤ 7. The per-prime form asks, at each conjugation-fixed prime, for some c < 1/5 there.
  • Lean. Collapse.chromaticNumber_planeOver_eq_plane_of_thick, Collapse.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt635087, Collapse.chromaticNumber_planeOver_deGrey, Collapse.chromaticNumber_planeOver_sqrt635087_six_or_seven, Collapse.chromaticNumber_planeOver_eq_plane_of_forall_thickAt.
  • Axioms. The general form: std3 + EL. The two fields and the per-prime form: std3 + EL + W. The corollary: std3 + EL + W + O5.
  • Whose. The derivation is this project's; the field equality: prior art not found in print. The plane's number it is equated with is [OAIMath2026]'s χ_m⁰; in dimension at least four the analogue is [Davies2026] Thm 1.1. Weil's bound gives the two fields' thickness (their conjugation-fixed primes lie above 103 and above 131 at the least).
  • Ledger. Row 128. Its density form (row 129's field clause) is proved by hand and not yet in Lean.

6. The density equality for the plane

  • Statement. α₁(ℝ²) = α₁(𝒜²) = m₁(ℝ²): the infimum of α(G)/|G| over finite point sets of the plane, and of the algebraic plane, equals the supremum of the upper densities of measurable sets with no two points at distance 1. Also χ_f(ℝ²) = χ_f(𝒜²) = 1/m₁(ℝ²), the supremum of χ_f over finite subgraphs of the plane is 1/m₁(ℝ²), and f(n)/n → m₁(ℝ²).
  • Lean. OneClass.finIndepRatio_plane_eq_mOne, OneClass.finIndepRatio_algPlane_eq_mOne, OneClass.fractionalChromaticNumber_plane, OneClass.fractionalChromaticNumber_algPlane, OneClass.iSup_fractionalChromaticNumber_plane, OneClass.tendsto_minIndepNum_div.
  • Axioms. std3.
  • Whose. For the plane: prior art not found in print. [Davies2026] Thm 1.1 proves the same equality in dimensions four and up. In the plane, f(n)/n → m₁(ℝ²) is the first clause of [DuczVarga2026]'s Conjecture 1, and χ_f(ℝ²) = 1/m₁(ℝ²) is the equality that [MatolcsiEtAl2025] § 2 calls not known (posed earlier by [Johnson1996fcn] and [Johnson2000surprises]). The rigidity step is [OAIMath2026]'s, ported. The kernel proof is this project's.
  • Ledger. Row 129.

7. Six against seven as a stability question

  • Statement. 7 ≤ χ(ℝ²) ⟺ ⨆_{ε > 0} µ₆(ε) < 1. Because µ₆(ε) grows as ε decreases, the supremum is the limit as ε → 0.
  • Lean. Outside.OAI158.seven_le_chromaticNumber_plane_iff.
  • Axioms. std3 (through [OAIMath2026]'s transfer theorem, its proof ported).
  • Whose. Prior art: not found in print as an equivalence. The conditional form is in [SokolovVoronov2025] § 9.
  • Ledger. Row 131.

8. The disc criterion

  • Statement. 7 ≤ χ(ℝ²) exactly when some open disc about the origin has no weak six-colouring: 7 ≤ χ(ℝ²) ⟺ ∃ R > 0, ¬ ∃ c, IsWeakColouringOn (ball 0 R) c. For every k, the plane is k-colourable exactly when every open disc about the origin has a weak k-colouring. If the plane is not k-colourable, some radius R has no c proper at almost every unit pair of any disc of radius R.
  • Lean. Plane.seven_le_chromaticNumber_plane_iff_exists_ball, Plane.colorable_iff_forall_ball, Plane.exists_radius_of_not_colorable.
  • Axioms. std3. The direction toward seven passes through [OAIMath2026]'s transfer, whose proof is ported.
  • Whose. The disc form for weak colourings: prior art not found in print. For a colouring of the whole plane the motion argument is [GwynStavrianos2022] Lemma 2; the bounded case for proper colourings is Kruskal (2008); the direction toward seven is [OAIMath2026]'s transfer, restricted to a disc.
  • Ledger. Row 161.

9. The p-adic plane

  • Statement. For every prime p ≡ 3 (mod 4) and every proper colouring of the p-adic plane with k measurable colour classes, 1 + (p + 1)/(2√p) ≤ k; so ⌈1 + (p + 1)/(2√p)⌉ ≤ χ_Bor(ℚ_p²), and no constant bounds χ_Bor(ℚ_p²) over these p. χ_Bor(ℚ_p²) is 3, 4, 5, 5 at p = 3, 7, 11, 19, and 5 ≤ χ_Bor(ℚ₂₃²) ≤ 8.
  • Lean. PadicPlane.one_add_div_le_of_borelColoring, PadicPlane.le_borelChromaticNumber_padicPlane, PadicPlane.not_exists_bound_borelChromaticNumber_padicPlane; PadicPlane.borelChromaticNumber_padicPlane_three (and …_seven, …_eleven, …_nineteen), PadicPlane.five_le_borelChromaticNumber_padicPlane_twentyThree, PadicPlane.borelChromaticNumber_padicPlane_twentyThree_le_eight. Level two at 11 on its own: chromaticNumber_unitQuadrance_zmod121, χ(UD(ℤ/121, 2)) = 5.
  • Axioms. The bound, the unboundedness and the value at 3: std3 + W. The values at 7, 11 and 19, the bounds at 23 and the level-two value at 11: std3.
  • Whose. The question is [BardestaniMallahiKarai2017] Question 1, whose authors prove O(p²) from above. The lower bound and the answer: prior art not found in print. The route is Hoffman's ratio bound on every finite level of the residue tower; it does not use their Theorem 2.5. The upper halves are [Madore2015]'s construction with the finite planes' values ([Moorhouse2010] Table 6.1). The lower halves: prior art not found in print. The plain chromatic number of ℚ_p² is not claimed; 6 ≤ χ_Bor(ℚ₂₃²) rests on an exact certificate outside the kernel.
  • Ledger. Row 75; CLOSED-QUESTIONS.md § 1.8.

10. Three-colourings of lattice Cayley graphs (by hand)

  • Statement. For Λ finitely generated torsion-free abelian and S = −S finite generating: Cay(Λ, S) is 3-colourable ⟺ some θ ∈ Hom(Λ, ℝ/ℤ) has θ(S) ⊆ [1/3, 2/3] ⟺ it has a periodic 3-colouring; periodic 3-colourings are dense; the colouring space is finite exactly when every cell of that set of θ is a point.
  • Lean. The direction "3-colourable ⟹ such a θ", for every abelian group and step set: ThreeColourCharacter.bandSet_three_nonempty_of_proper. The rest is not formalized.
  • Axioms. That direction: std3.
  • Whose. Proved by hand, two independent derivations. The questions are [Ellenberg2018]'s, Gibbs's and Mixon's (Polymath16), [CGHO2025bg] Question 6.2, and [AbrishamiEtAl2025] Problem 4.6 at three colours. The height lift is Chandgotia's device; density at the standard generators is expected folklore and not claimed. The criterion for arbitrary generators: prior art not found in print.
  • Ledger. Row 79; CLOSED-QUESTIONS.md § 2.4.

Other people's results, checked here

  • Whose. Classical (the Moser spindle); the kernel proof checks all 3⁷ colourings. Ledger. Rung A (CLAUDE.md § The mission).

The plane needs at least five colours, and at most seven

  • Whose. The graphs and the bound are de Grey's (2018) and Heule's. The kernel check, through an LRAT checker the kernel can run, is this project's. The seven-colouring is classical: "The colouring is classical; ours is the certificate." Ledger. Rung B, row 33.

The density bound

  • Statement. Every Lebesgue-measurable planar set with no two points at distance 1 has upper density at most 0.2470 about every centre; so m₁(ℝ²) ≤ 0.2470 < 1/4.
  • Lean. Plane.oneAvoidingDensityBound, Plane.mOne_le_0_2470, Plane.mOne_lt_one_quarter — axioms std3.
  • Whose. [AmbrusEtAl2023] Theorem 1. The proof starts from the paper's own certificate and needs no Bessel-function citation; the route, the formalization and the kernel check are this project's. Ledger. Row 129.

χ(ℚ(√3, √11)²) = 4

  • Lean. chromaticNumber_seamPlane ("Theorem B" in the project's records) — axioms std3.
  • Whose. The value is [Fischer1994]'s. The kernel proof is this project's, by a residue colouring with values in 𝔽₄, which is a different colouring from his. Ledger. Row 57.

The upper bound by finite planes

  • Statement. χ(F²) ≤ L(F) for every real number field. Also χ(F²) ≤ χ(UD(𝔽_p, 2)), the unit-distance graph of the finite plane 𝔽_p², at any prime of F whose residue field is 𝔽_p with p ≡ 3 (mod 4).
  • Lean. UpperHalf.chromaticNumber_planeOver_le_localBound, UpperHalf.chromaticNumber_planeOver_le_finitePlane — axioms std3.
  • Whose. [Moorhouse2010] §8.2/§10.3, [Manturov2012] Thms 17–19 and [Madore2015] Cor. 3.4. The kernel proof is this project's. Ledger. Row 52.

χ(ℚ²) = 2

  • Lean. UpperHalf.chromaticNumber_planeOver_rat — axioms std3.
  • Whose. Woodall's value ([Woodall1973]); the mechanism is in [Fischer1990] Thm 8 and [Moorhouse2010] Thm 7. Ledger. Row 63.

Adding a transcendental changes nothing

  • Statement. χ(F(t)²) = χ(F²) for a subfield F ⊆ ℝ and t transcendental over it.
  • Lean. RouteC.chromaticNumber_planeOver_adjoin_transcendental_eq — axioms std3.
  • Whose. [Fischer1990] Thm 6. The kernel proof is this project's. Ledger. Row 104.

The outside theorems: three assumed, two ported

  • W, StandingFive.kloosterman_bound (an axiom): [Weil1948] p. 207. Over a finite field of characteristic not 2, with ψ a non-trivial additive character and 4cd = a ≠ 0, ‖Σ_{x≠0} ψ(cx + dx⁻¹)‖ ≤ 2√q.
  • EL, Rigidity.positive_entropy_rigidity (an axiom): [EinsiedlerLindenstrauss2022] Theorem 1.3, positive-entropy rigidity for ℤ^d-actions (d ≥ 2) on solenoids with no virtually cyclic factors.
  • O5, Outside.OAI158.no_proper_five_coloring (an axiom): [OAIMath2026] family 158, no c : ℂ → Fin 5 with c x ≠ c y whenever ‖x − y‖ = 1. The axiom's statement is the release's stored statement, term for term.
  • Outside.OAI158.proper_to_borel_weak (a theorem, its proof ported; std3): [OAIMath2026]'s transfer — a proper k-colouring of the plane gives a measurable one proper at almost every unit pair.
  • Outside.OAI158.Spectral.residual_fourier (a theorem, its proof ported; std3): [OAIMath2026]'s rigidity step, its Theorem 2.3 composed with the spectral theorem.

Each axiom is stated in its source's own form, read back against the source, has a model of its hypotheses in its file, and is listed in ARCHITECTURE.md § 5. What landing each in Lean would mean is ROADMAP.md § 2.
