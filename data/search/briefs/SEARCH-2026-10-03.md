**Definitions you need.** Γ_q is the unit-distance graph on 𝔽_q²: vertices the points of 𝔽_q², an edge between two points whose difference
(x, y) has x² + y² = 1 (`CLAUDE.md` § Notation). U_Q = Cay(𝔽_{Q²}, μ_{Q+1}) is the Cayley graph on the field of Q² elements whose
connection set is the (Q+1)-st roots of unity. UD(ℤ/121, 2) is the unit-distance graph on (ℤ/121)², an edge where x² + y² ≡ 1 (mod 121). A
vertex's closed neighbourhood is the vertex and its neighbours; a "gap colour" at a vertex is a colour absent from its closed neighbourhood.
The project's kernel already holds proper colourings of some of these graphs, which make good controls: `circ7Table` for Γ₇ (proper, a
colour missing at 14 of 49 vertices — `tools/fs129_ma2_fangap.py gamma7` prints it), and the two Γ₁₁ tables and the Γ₁₉ table used by
`tools/fs129_oa1_falconer_fan.py` and `tools/fs129_ma1_falcfan.py`. Build each graph yourself from its definition, and check it against the
held table's graph where one exists.
