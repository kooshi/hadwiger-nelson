HD1 — character colourings cannot give the field-level "no".
- Claim: at the field level, Θ₄(F) (characters θ of R with θ(U_F) ⊆ [1/4, 3/4] + ℤ) has no point with nonzero archimedean part. So FS-83's character colourings cannot supply the non-periodic colouring that would prove F(R) = 0.
- (a) A character of R with a nontrivial component at a non-stable (split) place has dense range on U_F. Unit vectors there have unbounded valuation (T(F_v) ≅ F_v^×), so {βa + γ/a}_p runs over ℤ[1/p]/ℤ. Hence θ = τ + ℓ, with τ torsion from the stable places and ℓ archimedean.
- (b) Weak approximation makes the τ-coordinate and the archimedean coordinate of u ∈ U_F independent. The archimedean range of ℓ on the dense set is a symmetric interval [−r, r], so τ(U_F) + [−r, r] ⊆ [1/4, 3/4] needs, if r > 0, τ(U_F) ⊆ (1/4, 3/4) strictly.
- (c) Such a τ is an additive homomorphism of the product of local planes into a circular clique K_{n/k} with 3 < n/k < 4. By the MIS-normal argument, its composition with a four-colouring is pulled back from the dyadic plane. The remaining check, as its author left it, is whether an additive circular colouring of P_m × H with ratio < 4 exists (K₄ at level one has χ_c = 4; the author expects none).
- (d) If none exists, Θ₄(F) is torsion only (Fischer-type, local at 2, periodic), and FS-83 at k = 4 over ℚ(√59) gives nothing toward F(R) = 0.
- Basis: derived for (a)–(b); prior for (c).
- Falsifier: an additive map R → ℤ/n with every unit vector landing in (n/4, 3n/4), n/k₀ ∈ (3, 4); or a character with a non-stable-place component and bounded range on U_F.
- Poros: bullet (a) is stated place by place, but a character of R is defined only modulo the global dual lattice. A torsion character of a stable place is itself represented with nonzero components at the archimedean place and at finitely many non-stable places. So "a component at a non-stable place" needs a definition before (a) can be proved.

HD2 — F(R) = 0 through gaps on a cofinal chain.
- Claim, part 1: F(R) = 0 iff F(Λ_M) = 0 for every M in a cofinal chain. Poros: this is B1's V1, now proved.
- Claim, part 2: F(Λ) = 0 whenever Λ has one periodic four-colouring with a fan gap (row 66, slack_addCayley_of_fanGap).
- Route: show every Λ_M has a gapped periodic colouring. The natural source is its sparse dyadic sub-tower Q_m past the saturation depth, where U cannot fill S_m, tightness may fail, and a gap may appear.
- A uniform-in-M construction, by formula and not by search, would prove that no finite triangle-free unit-distance graph over ℚ(√59) forces a pair at four colours. With the frame, it would also give a counterexample to P = F at four colours.
- Basis: derived for the equivalence and the gap consequence; prior for "sparse levels carry gaps".
- Falsifier: a Λ_M all of whose periodic four-colourings are gap-free at every level.
- Poros: two cautions.
  - Addendum 1's B2 T1 already says every 128Λ-periodic four-colouring of Λ₅₁₀ is a P₄-pullback, hence gap-free. So a gapped periodic colouring of Λ₅₁₀ needs dyadic period beyond 2⁷, or an odd prime where Λ's image is not the full circle (Λ₅₁₀ refuses at every odd prime below 29 by theorem).
  - A search for such a colouring is banned this week, so formula only.

HD3 — twisted dyadic homomorphisms bound P on a lattice.
- Claim: for finitely generated Λ, P(Λ) ⊆ ⋂ φ⁻¹(𝔓⁴), over all additive φ: Λ → O with φ(U) ⊆ S (each φ gives colourings pulled back from P₄ along φ).
- At the field level, φ(S) ⊆ S on the closure leaves only the symmetries. For finite U, the conditions are finitely many 2-adic equations N(φ(u)) = 1 on a rank-4 ℤ₂-linear map, so twists may exist and cut P(Λ) below Λ ∩ 𝔓⁴. Computing the 2-adic points mod 𝔓^m is exact computer algebra, not a colouring search.
- Basis: derived for the bound; prior for "twists exist".
- Falsifier: for Λ₅₁₀, the only solutions mod 𝔓^m (m ≤ 8) are the symmetries of the inclusion.
- Poros: this is lattice-level (P(Λ)), so it scores at most as Draw (lattice) and never as a field result.

HD4 — Kempe screens are blind at the field level.
- Claim: on Cay(R, U_F), every two-colour Kempe component of a colouring pulled back from P₄ spans its fibre. The voltage lattice contains u − u′ for every pair u ≡ u′ (mod 𝔓⁴) of unit vectors (walk 0 → u → u − u′), and with U_F dense these generate R ∩ 𝔓⁴ (or a finite-index part). So FS-70's voltage-escape screen cannot produce the "no" at the field level; escapes, if any, are non-Kempe.
- Basis: derived for the walk; prior for the generation.
- Falsifier: a Kempe component of a pulled-back colouring of R that misses part of its 𝔓⁴-fibre.
