  - The consequence claimed: HD1, if true, has a finite exact certificate (the Θ₄ cells of `Λ₅₁₀` and a saturation), which is a post-ban computation. If HD1 is false, its witness gives a non-periodic four-colouring of ℚ(√59)'s plane, so `F(R) = 0`.
- **VD-C (Theorem C, as downgraded by its own side).** Let `E ⊂ ℚ₂(√3)` be non-real, embedded compatibly with conjugation, with no odd stable place whose residue field is `𝔽₃`, `𝔽₇`, `𝔽₁₁` or `𝔽₁₉` (for example ℚ(√−709)).
