# Finite Plane Atlas

Published artifact (private until shared): **https://claude.ai/artifact/6GhLV6m6xmmVP8YVJoKZtn** (2026-09-16).

The finite Euclidean planes `UD(𝔽_q, 2)` for `q ≡ 3 (mod 4)` up to 83: the plane drawn from its circle, its spectrum and Hoffman
bound computed in the page, and a table of what is known about its chromatic number and independence number. `planes.html` is
self-contained (no build step) and is the published file.

**Where its values come from** (the `FACTS` table in the page): `data/fs53/window.md` (per-plane floors, ceilings and independence
bounds, with trust tags), `data/fplane/map.json` (`planes`, each value with its record), `data/fs50/README.md` (83). Checked current on
2026-09-17: 23 at 6–8 (five refused, FS-56), 43 at 6–8, 47 at 6–10, 59/67/79/83 at ≥ 7, 31 still 5–8, 19's refusal of four a solver
claim.
