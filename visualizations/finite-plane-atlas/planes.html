Atlas fields that reduce to it

    A field reduces to 𝔽_q when each of its square roots is a square modulo q or divisible by q; its plane then maps edge-to-edge into this one, so χ(F²) is at most this plane's χ.

    How this is drawn

    Grid places (x, y) at integer coordinates from −(q−1)/2 to (q−1)/2. An edge joins points whose difference (a, b) has a² + b² ≡ 1 (mod q); it is drawn as the shortest lift of (a, b), so edges that wrap around the torus leave the square.

    Rotation rings groups points by x² + y², one ring per value, and orders each ring by the rotation group of order q + 1, so every rotation turns every ring by the same angle.

    Bounds come from the lean-dir project's record. The Hoffman lines are computed here from the eigenvalues in floating point.
