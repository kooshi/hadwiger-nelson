Unit-Step Puzzle

Colour every cell of a grid that wraps around at its edges. Two cells clash when the step between them, (dx, dy), satisfies dx² + dy² ≡ 1 (mod ). Clashing cells need different colours.

Paint: tap or drag; tapping a cell that already has the chosen colour erases it. Inspect: tap a cell to see every cell it clashes with. Move: scroll the board when it is zoomed. Your work is kept in this browser, per level.
