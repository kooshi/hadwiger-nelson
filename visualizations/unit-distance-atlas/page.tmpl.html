The directions

    Any field

    Up to four square roots of positive integers. The search is exact; large fields at large N may stop at the step budget, and the page says so.

    How this is drawn

    Directions are every unit vector (x, y) whose coordinates are integer combinations of the field's square roots divided by N, found by exhaustive search in integer arithmetic.

    Ball draws all sums of at most r directions from the centre, nearest first, up to the point budget. Closure draws the unit star of the centre, then repeatedly the nearest point already joined to two drawn points.

    Edges are all pairs of drawn points at distance exactly 1. Floating point only proposes a pair; an integer identity decides it.

    χ above is the chromatic number of the whole plane over the field, as recorded in the lean-dir project; the drawing is a finite piece of it and is left uncoloured.
